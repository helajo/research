# 中文版

## 三行结论

1. **做空 MNQ / NQ 对冲 QQQ，几乎等于"少拿一点 QQQ，其余放国库券"。** 扣除 QQQ 的 0.18% 管理费后，被对冲的部分每年赚
   国库券加大约 0 到 0.3 个点（2023 到 2025 年高利率时约 0.4 到 0.5），没有资金费率收入，没有固定的爆仓线，交易成本每年约为
   名义价值的 0.01%。
2. **它抬高了永续合约要过的门槛。** 配方 A（空头名义价值为现货的 0.20，2 倍逐仓保证金）要在同样波动率下跑赢期货路线，
   资金费率在 2010 到 2026 年要高于约 3.4%，按最近三年的利率（国库券平均 4.45%）要高于约 7.9%；各交易所三个月的
   平均资金费率是 4.6% 到 7.5%，所以按最近三年的利率和最近的资金费率，期货路线略胜（按今天 4.04% 的国库券计算的门槛没有算）。
3. **它的代价是操作上的：** 需要一个能交易芝商所（CME）期货的美元保证金账户；一张 MNQ 约 5.7 万美元，规模小时只能粗调；
   纳指上涨时每天要付现金（2010 年以来最差的一个月，每 100 万美元现货、0.6 的对冲要付约 14.4 万美元）；税务取决于居住地。
   它不能对冲个股，也消除不了放在加密交易所的现货本身的托管风险。

## 编辑的决定

**值得加入报告，作为一节完整但简短的内容，而不是第四种策略。** 它是永续合约要打败的监管内基准：把永续的全部优势变成一个数字，
即资金费率要超过多少；按这把尺子，DEEP_DIVE 第 8 节"在 5% 费率、4.4% 利率下约为 0"的说法和五行结论第 5 点的盈亏平衡点都要
修改（最近三年配方 A 比期货少约 0.6 个点，门槛高 0.4 到 1 个点）。只放脚注会漏掉这两处更正；单列成策略又会夸大它，因为它
在经济上就是"少拿 QQQ"。

## 1. 期货路线是什么

**一句话：** 现货 QQQ 不动，在期货账户里卖出纳斯达克 100 指数期货。指数涨，期货亏、现货赚；指数跌，反过来。卖出的那部分
相当于把现货换成了国库券，再加一点期货价格里的融资利差。

**合约。** NQ 每个指数点 20 美元，微型合约 MNQ 每点 2 美元；最小变动 0.25 点（NQ 5 美元，MNQ 0.50 美元）；现金交割；
每季度到期（3、6、9、12 月的第三个星期五），按纳斯达克 100 的特别开盘报价（SOQ）结算；Globex 从周日下午 5 点到周五下午 4 点
（芝加哥时间）交易，每天 4 到 5 点暂停。所以每年要展期四次。按 2026-11-04 纳斯达克 100 收盘 28,437.17 点，一张 NQ 是
568,743 美元，一张 MNQ 是 56,874 美元，相当于约 82 股 QQQ。

**例子：10 万美元的 QQQ。**

| | 相当于配方 A 的对冲（现货的 0.60） | 全部对冲（1.0） |
|---|---|---|
| 需要的 MNQ | 1.05 张，取 1 张，实际对冲 0.57 | 1.76 张，取 2 张，实际对冲 1.14（多对冲 14%） |
| 要存的保证金 | 3,522 到 6,285 美元（现货的 3.5% 到 6.3%） | 7,044 到 12,570 美元（7.0% 到 12.6%） |
| 2010 年以来最差一天（QQQ +12.0%，2025-04-09）要付的现金 | 约 6,800 美元 | 约 13,700 美元 |
| 最差一个月（+24.0%，到 2020-04-15） | 约 13,600 美元 | 约 27,300 美元 |
| 最差一个季度（+45.0%，到 2020-06-22） | 约 25,600 美元 | 约 51,200 美元 |

对比：永续配方 A 在 10 万美元上要存 10,000 美元（现货的 10%）。每一笔期货亏损都对应现货的同等涨幅，但现金要在期货账户里付出。
保证金的范围来自不同来源和日期：IBKR 隔夜维持 3,275、开仓 4,638 美元（ProRealTime 转载，2026-10-30），LP Futures 隔夜
3,522 美元（2026-10-27），IB 隔夜 6,285 美元（emini-watch，2026-11-05，约名义价值的 11%）。表中以 LP Futures 的 3,522 美元
（约 6.2%）为下限；IBKR 的 3,275 美元是持仓时的维持水平，不是开仓要存的金额。保证金随时可以上调；芝商所自己的水平没有读到。

**规模。** 1 张 MNQ 是 10 万美元账户的 57%，25 万美元的 23%，100 万美元的 5.7%。要做到每档约 6% 的精细调整，大约需要
100 万美元（11 张对应 0.63，18 张对应 1.02）。NQ 一张 57 万美元，只适合更大的账户。

**和永续的关键区别。**
- **没有固定的爆仓线，但亏损没有上限。** 期货每天逐日盯市：纳指上涨，亏损当天用现金付；下跌，收益当天入账。永续 2 倍逐仓在
  TQQQ 一个月内涨约 46% 时清零；期货没有这条线，但空头要付完整的涨幅。
- **追加保证金不到位，券商会强平。** IB 的风险披露写明：一般会在不事先通知、不让客户选择的情况下平仓以满足保证金要求。IBKR 会在
  证券和期货两部分之间转移多余现金，但"自动用 QQQ 的浮盈借钱付期货亏损"没有来源支持。要在期货账户里留足现金缓冲。
- **没有交易所托管、自动减仓和下架风险，也没有资金费率收入。**
- **成本可以忽略：** IBKR 一张 MNQ 每边约 0.62 美元（佣金 0.25 美元为 ProRealTime 通过 IBKR 的报价，交易所费用约 0.36 美元），
  加半个最小变动价位的滑点约 0.87 美元；一年四次展期约为名义价值的 0.012%（NQ 0.007%）。永续 0.06% 的吃单费单看手续费约是
  MNQ 的 55 倍。期货和 QQQ 都随指数一比一变动，不需要像做空 TQQQ 那样每月重置。
- **基差风险小，而且没有持续的溢价对你不利。** QQQ 总回报和期货之间的月度差异约 0.20% 到 0.32%，每日 0.19% 到 0.26%
  （部分是两个收盘时间不同）；永续在美东 16:00 平均比 TQQQ 收盘价高 0.32%。

## 2. 持有成本：期货利差对资金费率

**期货的价格里含有一个融资利率。** 期货通常比"国库券减股息"的理论价略贵，卖空的一方以较高价格卖出，到期价格回到指数，于是
赚到这个利差。和直接卖出同样数量的 QQQ 相比，空头每年多赚这个利差，但继续付 QQQ 的管理费。

| 衡量 | 结果 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|
| 纳指期货相对 3 个月国库券的隐含利差（扣 QQQ 管理费前），2010 到 2026 年 | 约 0.25% 到 0.33% | financing_spread，核查者修正 |
| 同上，2024 到 2026 年 | 约 0.6% | 同上 |
| 空头赚到利差的季度合约比例 | 约 80%（按方法 75% 到 85%）；最差一张约 −0.9%/年（2019 年 3 月合约） | 同上 |
| 扣 QQQ 0.18% 管理费后，被对冲部分比国库券多赚，2010 到 2025 年 | 约 0 到 0.3 个点（最可能 0.1 到 0.25） | mechanics、backtest、practical 三个估计 |
| 同上，2023 到 2025 年 | 约 0.4 到 0.5 个点 | mechanics 核查者 |
| 现在（2026 年 12 月合约，2026-10-01 到 11-04） | 扣费前约国库券 + 0.3 到 0.5，扣费后 + 0.2 到 0.3 | mechanics 核查者（对比债券等价收益率 4.12%） |
| 外部核对：标普 500 期货隐含利率减 3 个月国债，2004 到 2017 年 | 平均 47.6 个基点 | NBER w26773 表 A.12 |

**非美国持有人还要扣股息预扣税。** 期货价格按指数的全部股息定价，但持有 QQQ 的人只拿到税后股息：新加坡居民 30%（美国与新加坡
没有税收协定，IRS 表 3），被对冲部分每年约少 0.13 个点；中国居民按协定 10%（IRS 表 1），约 0.045 个点。把利差、管理费、展期和
预扣税放在一起，期货路线相对"少拿 QQQ"每年：利差 0.35% 时 +0.14 / +0.07 / +0.01（预扣 0 / 15% / 30%），利差 0.60% 时
+0.39 / +0.32 / +0.26，利差为 0 时 −0.21 到 −0.34。对 0.20 的组合规模来说，整个组合每年约 −0.07 到 +0.08 个点。

**永续收的是资金费率。** 各交易所在 2026-08-06 到 11-04 的三个月平均年化资金费率是 4.6% 到 7.5%，币安上线以来 4.0%，约 +10%
只在 2026 年 10 月中旬以后出现过（DEEP_DIVE 第 2 节）。资金费率每多 1 个点，配方 A 每年多约 0.19 个点，1/3 对冲多约 0.28 到 0.29。
要注意：永续的资金费率是按 TQQQ 名义价值收的毛收入，DEEP_DIVE 的结论是最好的配方相对同波动率组合也只是打平；所以应该拿它的
净结果和期货比，而不是拿毛资金费率比。

**盈亏平衡的资金费率（永续要高于它才跑赢期货）：**

| 比较方法 | 2010-02 到 2026-10 | 2023-10 到 2026-10（国库券平均 4.45%） | 2000-09 到 2010-02 压力期 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 配方 A 对期货 k=0.60，同样波动率，利差 +0.25%（利差 0 到 0.5% 时的范围） | 3.4%（2.7% 到 4.2%） | 7.9%（7.1% 到 8.6%） | 1.6% |
| 配方 A，同样日度贝塔，对卖出 QQQ | 2.9% | 7.2% | |
| 配方 A，同样日度贝塔，对期货（利差 0.10% 到 0.25%） | 3.3% 到 3.8% | 7.6% 到 8.1% | |
| 配方 A 对期货 k=0.60，只比年化收益 | 1.5% | 6.2% | 3.2% |

按同一种方法，期货比"卖出 QQQ"把门槛抬高 0.4 到 1 个点（取决于利差）。7.9% 是按最近三年的平均利率，不是按今天的利率
（数据里最后的 3 个月国库券是 4.04%，2026-10-27）；按今天利率的盈亏平衡点没有计算。7.9% 高于所有交易所三个月的均值。其中一部分
来自一个假设：永续的保证金不生息，而期货的保证金赚国库券。

## 3. 回测比较（同样波动率）

模拟设置：每月重置；期货保证金为名义价值的 10%，另有 5% 现金储备，都赚国库券；永续用研究的模拟器（维持保证金 2.5%，吃单费 0.06%，
保证金不生息，5% 储备）。"相对组合"是相对同样波动率的"QQQ 加国库券"组合，单位为年化百分点。k 是期货空头占现货的比例；
k=0.60 和配方 A 的市场暴露相当，k=1.0 是全部对冲。

|---|---|---|
| 期货 k=0.60，利差 0 | 7.87% / 7.30% / −13.4% / −0.12 / 追加保证金 1.0 | 12.81% / 7.26% / −8.3% / −0.09 / 1.3 |
| 期货 k=0.60，利差 +0.25% | 8.02% / 7.30% / −13.3% / +0.02 / 1.0 | 12.96% / 7.26% / −8.3% / +0.06 / 1.3 |

| 组合：期货 k=0.60 的对照（QQQ 35.4% / 35.5%） | 7.99%，最大回撤 −12.7% | 12.90%，最大回撤 −8.3% |
| 组合：配方 A 的对照（QQQ 37.4% / 36.8%） | 8.35%，最大回撤 −13.5% | 13.23%，最大回撤 −8.6% |
| 组合：1/3 对冲的对照（QQQ 10.9% / 9.9%） | 3.53%，最大回撤 −3.75% | 6.79%，最大回撤 −1.96% |
| QQQ / 国库券（年化） | QQQ 19.27%；国库券 1.51% | QQQ 28.32%；国库券 4.45% |

怎么读：
- **期货 k=0.60 就是"少拿 QQQ"。** +0.02 在模型误差之内（独立重算是 +0.08），而且完全取决于假设的 +0.25% 利差；利差为 0 时的
  −0.12 大部分（−0.13）只是对照组合每天再平衡、期货账户每月重置造成的，不是期货的成本。期货的最大回撤（−13.3%）比组合（−12.7%）
  略深。真实 NQ=F 价格的年化收益落在利差 0 和 +0.25% 的模型之间；它的波动率被期货和 QQQ 收盘时间不同放大了，所以只看年化。
- **配方 A 相对期货：** 资金费率 +5% 时 2010 到 2026 年多约 0.2 个点（按比较方法 0.17 到 0.23），最近三年少约 0.6 到 0.7 个点；
  +10% 时多约 1.2 和 0.4。
- **全部对冲（k=1.0）就是合成的国库券加利差**：1.66% 对国库券 1.51%（最近三年 4.69% 对 4.45%）。1/3 永续对冲的结果取决于比较方法：
  按同样日度贝塔，+5% 时比期货多 0.3（2010 到 2026）、少 0.9（最近三年）；按同样波动率则少约 0.85 和 2.4。只比年化时它在资金费率
  高于 0.6% / 5.2% / 2.4%（2010 到 2026 / 最近三年 / 压力期）时胜过 k=1.0，同波动率时是 7.2% / 12.5% / −0.2%；两种方法差距很大，
  都不是可靠的门槛。
- **压力期 2000-09 到 2010-02**（QQQ 年化 −7.3%）：期货 k=0.60 年化 0.1%，最大回撤 −36.1%，比组合少 0.08，每年追加保证金 2.3 次；
  k=1.0 年化 2.65%（国库券 2.45%），5.4 次。永续配方 A 在资金费率 0 / 5% / 10% 时 −0.5% / 0.4% / 1.2%，最大回撤 −38.4% / −37.4% /
  −36.4%（用重建的 TQQQ，只按收盘价判断爆仓）。MNQ 何时上市没有核对，这段时间可能只有 NQ。
- **期货的实际负担都很小：** 模型里 k=0.60 每年约 1 次追加保证金（按开仓保证金检查，偏保守），k=1.0 约 4 次；为此卖出的现货每年约为
  期初净值的 11% 和 23%，月底重置时买回。保证金现金不生息时，k=0.60 的年化少 0.09（2010 到 2026）和 0.27 个点（最近三年）。
  模型假设每次追加都在收盘时卖出现货补足，这要求现货和期货在同一账户、可以互相抵用，没有建模。
- 配方 A 是在同一段样本上选出的，最近三年只相当于约一段独立的时期。

## 4. 四条路线并排

| 路线 | 被对冲部分赚什么 | 主要风险 | 工作量 | 适合谁 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. 卖出部分 QQQ | 现金的国库券利息 | 卖出时的税（新加坡一般没有） | 最小 | 能卖的人 |
| 2. 做空 NQ / MNQ | 国库券加约 0 到 0.3 个点（扣 QQQ 管理费后；近年约 0.4 到 0.5） | 上涨时每天付现金，追加不到位被券商强平；每季度展期；合约规模粗；要另开账户 | 设好之后很低 | 不能卖或不想卖的人；可以随时加减的临时对冲；分散的、像纳指的组合 |
| 3. 做空 TQQQ 永续 | 约国库券加资金费率，减爆仓和交易所成本 | 交易所 / USDT 托管、自动减仓（恰好在对冲起作用时）、下架、固定爆仓线（2 倍、2.5% 维持保证金时 TQQQ 一个月涨约 46%）、资金费率可能转负；中国居民的合规问题 | 高：每月重置、续杯、留意分档 | 只适合相信资金费率会稳定高于门槛（按最近三年利率约 8%）并接受交易所风险的人 |
| 4. 买 SQQQ 或认沽期权 | 要付钱 | SQQQ 有磨损；期权有时间价值损耗 | 低 | 想要亏损有上限、没有追加保证金的人 |

第 4 条的数字：买相当于现货 0.20 的 SQQQ 年化 5.9%（比同波动率组合少 1.6），0.33 为 −0.7%（少 5.2）。芝加哥期权交易所的标普 500
保护性认沽指数（PPUT）2010 到 2026 年年化 10.57%，标普 500 总回报 14.14%；长期窗口里每年落后 2.8 到 3.6 个点，最近三年落后 5.0；
但相对同样波动率的标普加国库券组合（10.9%）只少约 0.3，最大回撤 −22.1%（指数 −33.8%，组合 −25.5%）。纳指认沽隐含波动率更高，
会更贵，但没有测算。

**个股不在此列。** 用 QQQ 或 NQ 对冲单只大型科技股，样本外只能降低 18% 到 42% 的日波动（TSLA 18%、META 27%、AVGO 31%、
GOOGL 和 AMZN 35%、NVDA 36%、AAPL 38%、MSFT 42%），最大回撤的变化在 −9 到 +13 个点之间，没有规律，有时更差（MSFT 从 −37%
变成 −46%）。8 只大型股的等权组合则去掉约 69% 的波动，最大回撤从 −46% 减半到 −22%。2023 年以来，QQQ 对 MSFT、AAPL、GOOGL、
AMZN、META 日涨跌的解释力比 2016 到 2019 年和 2020 到 2022 年都低。DEEP_DIVE 里"NVDA / MSFT / AVGO / TSLA 在任何设置下都输"
那几行，指数期货同样解决不了。

## 5. 谁该用哪一条

- **能卖、卖出不产生税（例如一般情况下的新加坡个人投资者）：** 卖出部分 QQQ，持有国库券。结果和期货几乎一样，工作最少。
- **卖出会产生税、现货不能卖（锁定期、员工持股）、或者想做可以随时加减的临时对冲：** 期货。前提是组合像纳指一样分散，
  并且在期货账户里留足现金缓冲。一次 MNQ 开平的手续费约为名义价值的 0.003%。
- **永续：** 只有在你认为资金费率会长期高于门槛（2010 到 2026 年约 3.4%，按最近三年利率约 7.9%），并接受交易所、自动减仓、下架和
  托管风险时才有意义。按最近的利率和 4.6% 到 7.5% 的三个月平均资金费率，它不如期货。
- **想要亏损有上限、不想面对追加保证金：** 认沽期权，代价是每年几个点。
- **持有的是单只股票：** 任何指数对冲都只能去掉一小部分风险；卖出一部分才是真正降低风险的办法。

**换成期货不会消除现货本身的"交易所风险"。** 如果现货放在加密交易所，期货只把对冲腿的自动减仓、下架、爆仓线和托管风险拿走，
现货的托管还在原处。如果现货是 xStocks 的 QQQx 这类代币，持有人没有股东权利，只能通过发行方 Backed 赎回（另收费用；二手资料说
可能只对合格投资者或有最低金额）。两条腿分在两个地方还会产生新问题：上涨时期货在券商那里亏现金，而浮盈在加密交易所，卖出再电汇
要几天。这些投资人的现货是代币还是券商账户里的 ETF，材料里没有说明。

# English version

## The answer in three lines

1. **Shorting MNQ / NQ against QQQ is almost exactly "hold less QQQ and keep the rest in T-bills".** After QQQ's 0.18% fee
   the hedged part earns the T-bill rate plus roughly 0 to 0.3 points a year (about 0.4-0.5 in the high-rate years
   2023-2025), with no funding income, no fixed wipe-out level, and trading costs of about 0.01% of notional a year.
2. **It raises the bar the perpetual has to clear.** For recipe A (short TQQQ worth 0.20 of spot, 2x isolated margin) to
   beat the futures route at the same volatility, funding must exceed about 3.4% a year over 2010-2026 and about 7.9% at the
   last three years' rates (T-bills averaging 4.45%). The venues' three-month average funding was 4.6-7.5%, so at recent
   rates and recent funding the futures route edges ahead (the hurdle at today's 4.04% T-bill was not computed).
3. **Its costs are practical:** a USD margin account with access to CME futures; contracts of about $57k, so small books can
   only size coarsely; cash paid out daily when the Nasdaq rises (about $144k per $1M of spot at a 0.6 hedge in the worst
   month since 2010); and tax rules that depend on where you live. It does not hedge single stocks, and it does not remove the
   custody risk of spot held on a crypto exchange.

## The editor's decision

**Worth adding, as a full but short section, not as a fourth strategy.** It is the regulated yardstick the perpetual has to
beat, and it turns the perpetual's whole case into one number: how much funding it needs. Measured with it, two statements in
DEEP_DIVE change (section 8's "roughly zero at 5% funding and 4.4% rates" and the break-even in point 5 of the five-line
answer: over the last three years recipe A trails futures by about 0.6 points, and the hurdle is 0.4-1 point higher), which a
footnote would miss, while a strategy chapter would oversell it, because economically it is holding less QQQ.

## 1. What the futures route is

**In one sentence:** keep the QQQ, and in a futures account sell Nasdaq-100 index futures. When the index rises the futures
lose and the spot gains; when it falls, the reverse. The part you sell works like swapping spot for T-bills, plus a small
financing spread built into the futures price.

**The contract.** NQ pays $20 per index point and the micro MNQ $2; the tick is 0.25 points ($5 on NQ, $0.50 on MNQ); cash
settled; quarterly expiries on the third Friday of March, June, September and December, settled at the Nasdaq-100 Special
Opening Quotation (SOQ); Globex trades from Sunday 5 pm to Friday 4 pm Chicago time with a daily 4-5 pm halt. So you roll four
times a year. At the Nasdaq-100 close of 28,437.17 on 2026-11-04, one NQ is $568,743 and one MNQ $56,874, about 82 QQQ shares.

**Example: US$100,000 of QQQ.**

| | Same exposure as recipe A (0.60 of spot) | Full hedge (1.0) |
|---|---|---|
| MNQ needed | 1.05, so 1 contract, giving 0.57 | 1.76, so 2 contracts, giving 1.14 (14% over-hedged) |
| Margin posted | $3,522-6,285 (3.5-6.3% of spot) | $7,044-12,570 (7.0-12.6%) |
| Cash paid on the worst day since 2010 (QQQ +12.0%, 2025-04-09) | about $6,800 | about $13,700 |
| Worst month (+24.0%, to 2020-04-15) | about $13,600 | about $27,300 |
| Worst quarter (+45.0%, to 2020-06-22) | about $25,600 | about $51,200 |

For comparison, perpetual recipe A posts $10,000 (10% of spot) on the same book. Every futures loss is matched by an equal gain
on the spot, but the cash has to be paid into the futures account. The margin range spans sources and dates: IBKR overnight
maintenance $3,275 and initial $4,638 (reproduced by ProRealTime, 2026-10-30), LP Futures overnight $3,522 (2026-10-27), IB
overnight $6,285 (emini-watch, 2026-11-05, about 11% of notional). The table uses LP Futures' $3,522 (about 6.2%) as the floor;
IBKR's $3,275 is the level to hold the position, not the amount posted to open it. Margins can be raised at any time; CME's own
level was not read.

**Size.** One MNQ is 57% of a $100k book, 23% of $250k and 5.7% of $1M. Sizing in steps of about 6% needs about $1M (11
contracts give 0.63, 18 give 1.02). NQ, at $569k a contract, suits only larger books.

**How it differs from the perpetual.**
- **No fixed wipe-out level, but no cap on the loss.** Futures are marked to market daily: a Nasdaq rise is paid in cash that
  day, a fall is credited. The perpetual at 2x isolated margin is wiped out if TQQQ rises about 46% within a month; futures have
  no such line, but the short pays the whole rally.
- **If a margin call is not met, the broker closes the position.** IB's risk disclosure says it generally liquidates to meet
  margin requirements without prior notice and without letting the client choose. IBKR moves excess cash between its securities
  and futures segments, but no source supports "it will automatically borrow against the QQQ gain to pay the futures loss". Keep a
  cash buffer in the futures account.
- **No exchange custody, auto-deleveraging or delisting risk, and no funding income.**
- **Costs are negligible:** about $0.62 per MNQ per side at IBKR (a $0.25 commission as ProRealTime quotes it through IBKR, plus
  about $0.36 of exchange fees), $0.87 with half a tick of slippage; four rolls a year come to 0.012% of notional (NQ 0.007%).
  On fees alone, the perpetual's 0.06% taker fee is about 55 times the MNQ cost per side. Futures and QQQ both move one-for-one
  with the index, so there is no monthly reset as with a TQQQ short.
- **Basis risk is small and has no persistent premium against you.** The gap between QQQ total return and the futures varies by
  about 0.20-0.32% a month and 0.19-0.26% a day (partly because the two closes are taken at different times); the perpetual
  closes on average 0.32% above TQQQ at 16:00 ET.

## 2. What it costs to hold: the futures spread against funding

**The futures price contains a financing rate.** Futures usually trade a little above fair value (T-bills minus dividends).
The short sells at that richer price and the price converges to the index by expiry, so the short earns the spread. Compared
with selling the same amount of QQQ, the short earns the spread each year but keeps paying QQQ's fee.

| Measure | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nasdaq-100 futures implied spread over 3-month T-bills (before QQQ's fee), 2010-2026 | about 0.25-0.33% a year | financing_spread, verifier's correction |
| The same, 2024-2026 | about 0.6% | same |
| Share of quarterly contracts in which the short earned the spread | about 80% (75-85% by method); the worst about -0.9%/yr (March 2019 contract) | same |
| After QQQ's 0.18% fee, what the hedged part earns above T-bills, 2010-2025 | roughly 0 to 0.3 points (best guess 0.1-0.25) | mechanics, backtest, practical estimates |
| The same, 2023-2025 | about 0.4-0.5 points | mechanics verifier |
| Today (December 2026 contract, 2026-10-01 to 11-04) | T-bill + 0.3-0.5 before the fee, + 0.2-0.3 after | mechanics verifier (against a bond-equivalent T-bill of 4.12%) |
| Outside check: S&P 500 futures implied rate minus the 3-month Treasury, 2004-2017 | 47.6 bp on average | NBER w26773, Table A.12 |

**Non-US holders also lose dividend withholding.** The futures price allows for the full index dividend, but a QQQ holder keeps
only the dividend after US withholding: 30% for a Singapore resident (no US treaty, IRS Table 3), about 0.13 points a year on the
hedged amount; 10% for a PRC resident under the treaty (IRS Table 1), about 0.045 points. Putting spread, fee, rolls and
withholding together, the futures route against holding less QQQ is, per year: at a spread of 0.35%, +0.14 / +0.07 / +0.01 (0 /
15% / 30% withholding); at 0.60%, +0.39 / +0.32 / +0.26; at 0, -0.21 to -0.34. On a 0.20-sized hedge that is about -0.07 to
+0.08 points a year for the whole portfolio.

**The perpetual earns funding instead.** Over 2026-08-06 to 11-04 the venues' three-month average funding was 4.6-7.5% a year,
Binance's since listing 4.0%, and about +10% only since mid-October 2026 (DEEP_DIVE section 2). Each extra point of funding adds
about 0.19 points a year to recipe A and 0.28-0.29 to the one-third hedge. Note that funding is gross income on TQQQ notional,
and DEEP_DIVE finds the best recipe only a wash against the same-volatility mix; compare the perpetual's net result with the
futures route, not its gross funding.

**Break-even funding (the perpetual beats futures only above it):**

| Method | 2010-02 to 2026-10 | 2023-10 to 2026-10 (T-bills averaging 4.45%) | 2000-09 to 2010-02 stress |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recipe A against futures k=0.60, same volatility, spread +0.25% (range for spread 0 to 0.5%) | 3.4% (2.7-4.2%) | 7.9% (7.1-8.6%) | 1.6% |
| Recipe A, same daily beta, against selling QQQ | 2.9% | 7.2% | |
| Recipe A, same daily beta, against futures (spread 0.10-0.25%) | 3.3-3.8% | 7.6-8.1% | |
| Recipe A against futures k=0.60, plain CAGR | 1.5% | 6.2% | 3.2% |

Within one method, futures raise the hurdle over "sell QQQ" by 0.4-1 point, depending on the spread. The 7.9% is at the last
three years' average rate, not today's (the last 3-month T-bill in the data is 4.04%, 2026-10-27); the break-even at today's rate
was not computed. 7.9% is above every venue's three-month mean. Part of it comes from an assumption: the perpetual's margin earns
nothing while the futures margin earns T-bills.

  last three years). How the one-third perpetual hedge compares depends on the yardstick: at the same daily beta it is 0.3 ahead
  of futures at +5% over 2010-2026 and 0.9 behind over the last three years; at the same volatility it trails by about 0.85 and
  2.4. On plain CAGR it beats k = 1.0 above funding of 0.6% / 5.2% / 2.4% (2010-2026 / last three years / stress), on matched
  volatility above 7.2% / 12.5% / -0.2%; the methods disagree so much that neither is a firm hurdle.
- **The 2000-09 to 2010-02 stress** (QQQ -7.3% a year): futures k = 0.60 returned 0.1% a year, worst fall -36.1%, 0.08 below the
  mix, 2.3 margin calls a year; k = 1.0 returned 2.65% (T-bills 2.45%) with 5.4. Perp recipe A returned -0.5% / 0.4% / 1.2% at
  funding 0 / 5 / 10%, worst fall -38.4% / -37.4% / -36.4% (rebuilt TQQQ, liquidation checked on closes only). When MNQ was
  launched was not checked; for much of this window only NQ may have existed.
- **The futures' practical burdens are small:** in the model about 1 margin call a year at k = 0.60 (checked at initial margin,
  conservative) and about 4 at k = 1.0; the spot sold to meet them is about 11% and 23% of starting NAV a year, bought back at the
  monthly resets. If the margin cash earned nothing, k = 0.60 would lose 0.09 points of CAGR (2010-2026) and 0.27 (last three
  years). The model meets every call at the close by selling spot, which needs spot and futures cross-margined in one account;
  that is not modelled.
- Recipe A was chosen on the same sample, and the last three years hold about one independent period.

## 4. The four routes side by side

| Route | What the hedged part earns | Main risks | Effort | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sell some QQQ | T-bills on the cash | tax on the sale (generally none in Singapore) | minimal | anyone who can sell |
| 2. Short NQ / MNQ | T-bills plus roughly 0-0.3 points (after QQQ's fee; about 0.4-0.5 lately) | daily cash in rallies, broker close-out if a call is not met; quarterly rolls; coarse contract size; a second account | low once set up | those who cannot or will not sell; hedges put on and taken off; diversified Nasdaq-like books |
| 3. Short the TQQQ perpetual | about T-bills plus funding, minus liquidation and venue costs | exchange / USDT custody, auto-deleveraging (just when the hedge works), delisting, a fixed wipe-out level (TQQQ +46% within a month at 2x and 2.5% maintenance), funding that can turn negative; legality for PRC residents | high: monthly resets, refills, watching tiers | only those who expect funding to stay above the hurdle (about 8% at the last three years' rates) and accept venue risk |
| 4. Buy SQQQ or puts | you pay to hold it | SQQQ's decay; puts' time decay | low | those who want a capped loss with no margin calls |

Route 4 in numbers: SQQQ worth 0.20 of spot returned 5.9% a year (1.6 below its same-volatility mix), worth 0.33 -0.7% (5.2
below). Cboe's S&P 500 protective-put index (PPUT) returned 10.57% a year over 2010-2026 against 14.14% for the S&P 500 total
return: 2.8-3.6 points a year behind over long windows and 5.0 over the last three years, but only about 0.3 behind a same-volatility
S&P + T-bill mix (10.9%), with a worst fall of -22.1% (index -33.8%, mix -25.5%). Nasdaq-100 puts carry higher implied volatility
and would cost more; that was not measured.

**Single stocks are a different case.** Hedging one big tech stock with QQQ or NQ cut its daily volatility, out of sample, by only
18-42% (TSLA 18%, META 27%, AVGO 31%, GOOGL and AMZN 35%, NVDA 36%, AAPL 38%, MSFT 42%), and changed the worst fall unpredictably,
by -9 to +13 points, sometimes for the worse (MSFT from -37% to -46%). An equal-weight basket of eight megacaps lost about 69% of
its volatility and saw its worst fall halve, from -46% to -22%. Since 2023 QQQ explains less of MSFT's, AAPL's, GOOGL's, AMZN's and
META's daily moves than in either 2016-2019 or 2020-2022. DEEP_DIVE's single-stock lines (NVDA / MSFT / AVGO / TSLA lose at every
setting) would not be fixed by index futures either.

## 5. Who should use which

- **You can sell, and selling costs no tax (for example, Singapore individuals in general):** sell some QQQ and hold T-bills. Nearly
  the same result as futures, with the least work.
- **Selling triggers tax, the spot cannot be sold (lock-up, employee stock), or you want a hedge you can put on and take off:**
  futures, provided the book is diversified and Nasdaq-like and you keep a cash buffer in the futures account. One MNQ on-and-off
  round trip costs about 0.003% of notional.
- **The perpetual:** only if you believe funding will stay above the hurdle (about 3.4% over 2010-2026, about 7.9% at the last
  three years' rates) and accept exchange, auto-deleveraging, delisting and custody risk. At recent rates and the 4.6-7.5% three-month
  average funding it falls short of futures.
- **You want a capped loss and no margin calls:** puts, at a cost of a few points a year.
- **You hold a single stock:** no index hedge removes more than a minority of its risk; selling part of it is what reduces risk.

**Switching to futures does not remove the exchange risk on the spot.** If the spot sits on a crypto exchange, futures take
auto-deleveraging, delisting, the wipe-out level and perpetual custody off the hedge leg only; the spot's custody stays where it
is. If the spot is a token such as xStocks' QQQx, the holder has no shareholder rights, only redemption through the issuer, Backed
(for a fee; secondary sources say possibly only for qualified investors or above a minimum size). Splitting the legs also creates
a new problem: in a rally the futures lose cash at the broker while the gains sit on the crypto exchange, and selling and wiring
takes days. Whether these investors hold a token or a brokerage ETF is not stated in the material.

## 6. Access, size and tax (not tax advice)

**Singapore residents**
- Futures are Specified Investment Products: the broker must run a Customer Account Review (CAR) before enabling them. This rests
  on broker FAQs; the SGX page itself was not read. That IBKR Singapore is MAS-regulated is the workstream's statement; its licence
  page was not checked.
- Gains on shares and financial instruments are generally not taxable in Singapore (IRAS); if the activity is judged a trade (the
  "badges of trade", secondary sources only) gains can be taxed as income, a test that probably applies to futures and perpetuals
  alike.
- QQQ dividends lose 30% to US withholding (no US treaty, IRS Table 3): about 0.13 points a year on the hedged amount.
- Offshore crypto-derivative platforms are "outside of MAS' regulatory oversight" (MAS parliamentary reply, 2020): no Singapore
  protection on the perpetual route.
- US estate tax on US-listed assets held by non-US persons: unverified.

**Mainland China residents (the least certain part)**
- IBKR has tightened account opening for mainland residents since 2025: new clients must show a mainland ID and proof of overseas
  residence or employment (Yicai).
- The individual FX purchase quota is USD 50,000 a year (SAFE, 2007 page); that it may not be used for overseas securities rests
  only on 2017 reporting and is unverified.
- Overseas investment gains are taxed on net realised gains for the whole year (Caixin, confirmed by the Beijing tax hotline). The
  20% rate appears on Caixin's page only in an AI digest marked "for reference only"; check it against the Individual Income Tax Law.
  Caixin reports enforcement rising since mid-2024 with CRS data. How futures gains are classified was not confirmed.
- QQQ dividends lose 10% under the US-China treaty (IRS Table 1): about 0.045 points a year.
- The perpetual route is worse: notice 银发〔2026〕42号 (eight agencies, 2026-02-06, replacing 237号) restates that virtual-currency
  business is illegal financial activity. Whether a USDT-margined stock perpetual falls under it was not confirmed, but it plainly
  sits in the area the rules target.

**US persons, briefly:** NQ / MNQ are Section 1256 contracts, marked to market at year end, gains 60% long-term and 40% short-term.
That long QQQ against short index futures is "very likely" a Section 1092 mixed straddle is the workstream's own judgment; no
source says so directly. Treas. Reg. 1.1092(b)-6, Example 3, shows index-fund shares and a short index future elected as an
identified mixed straddle; Form 6781 boxes A (mixed straddle election) and B (straddle-by-straddle identification election) change
the result. Whether Section 1259 (constructive sale) applies turns on whether an index future is "substantially identical" to QQQ,
which no source resolved; a cash-settled index future is arguably not a contract "to deliver" QQQ at all. Ask a tax adviser.

## 7. What is still unknown

- The exact spread: 0 to 0.3 points after QQQ's fee is the range of several estimates, and single years can be negative; the
  backtest's +0.25% sits in the upper half of the evidence. The financing workstream's monthly series
  (`nq_implied_spread_monthly.csv`) has an expiry-day error and should not be used until it is fixed; its roll-handling claim was
  refuted and is dropped here.
- The break-even funding at today's T-bill rate (4.04%); whether perpetual margin can earn interest (which would lower the hurdle).
- CME's own margin levels (site returned 503) and IBKR's own commission page (403) were not read; margin figures are reproductions
  that disagree (6-11% of notional).
- Whether a broker will lend against QQQ automatically to meet a futures call; how cash moves between two venues in a rally.
- "Most traders roll a week before expiry" is unsourced; the 8:30 am Chicago last-trade time comes only from a search summary.
- Singapore's badges-of-trade test and US estate tax; China's classification of futures gains, the primary source for the 20%
  rate, whether the FX quota may fund overseas securities, and whether 42号 covers stock perpetuals.
- US: the "substantially identical" question under Section 1259; that offshore venues exclude US residents and that funding is
  ordinary income (both unverified).
- The real cost of Nasdaq-100 puts; QQQx redemption terms; whether the investors' spot is a token or an ETF; MNQ's launch date.
