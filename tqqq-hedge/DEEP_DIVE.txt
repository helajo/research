# 中文版

## 五行结论

1. **按交易所实际给出的保证金和资金费率**（维持保证金 2.5%，如币安和 Bitget；资金费率年化约 +5%），1/3 对冲的年化收益
   大约等于国库券（2010 到 2026 年 2.6% 到 2.9%），比"少拿一点 QQQ"的同波动率组合每年少 0.6 到 1.1 个点，最近三年
   少 2 到 3 个点。原报告的基准（1/3 对冲、5 倍杠杆）在这个条件下亏钱：年化 −0.7%，比组合少 5.1 个点，每年爆仓 4.5 次。
2. **唯一不明显输给"少拿 QQQ"的配方**：空头名义价值为现货的 0.20，2 倍逐仓保证金，每月重置。在 2.5% 维持保证金、+5%
   资金费率下，2010 到 2026 年比同波动率的 QQQ 加国库券组合多 0.3 个点，最近三年少 0.5 个点，基本打平；只有资金费率维持在
   +10% 左右才能多出约 1.3 个点，而这只在 2026 年 10 月中旬以后出现过。
3. **全部超额收益来自资金费率。** 基金的"磨损"（固定仓位持有一个月，比 −3 倍 QQQ 每年多赚名义价值的 7.4%）大部分是基金自己的
   管理费和融资成本（4.7 到 5.3 个点），按 1/3 对冲收到的钱和同样资金买国库券差不多；其余 2.0 到 2.7 个点是赌指数震荡，
   201 个月中有 80 个月为负。5 倍杠杆的爆仓每年约少赚 1 个点，不是原报告说的"损失 5% 到 6% 的净值"。
4. **如果还想做**：空头名义价值为现货的 0.20（或 0.25），2 倍逐仓保证金，每月重置，选在你的仓位大小下维持保证金不高于 2.5%
   的交易所（币安、Bitget 10 万 USDT 以内、MEXC 2%、OKX 2%、Gate 1%）。TQQQ 要在一个月内从上次重置价涨约 46% 到 48%
   才会爆仓（16.7 年里两次），5 倍杠杆是 17% 到 19%（每年三到四次）。不要在 KuCoin 的分档下做 5 倍：10 万美元的账户最多
   4 倍，年化 −9.9%。
5. **预期收益是国库券加上资金费率超过约 3% 的部分**（0.20 对冲、2 倍杠杆在 2010 到 2026 年的盈亏平衡点；按现在的利率是
   6% 到 7%），并接受 2010 年的 1 美元只变成 4.72 美元，而 QQQ 变成 18.89 美元。交易所、自动减仓、下架、税务和托管问题
   没有任何回测能定量，足以抹掉不到一个点的优势。

**资金费率的机制。** 币安的公式是：资金费率 = 溢价指数 + clamp(利率 − 溢价指数, ±0.05%)。股票永续的利率分量设为 0，
所以只要 8 小时平均溢价在 ±0.05% 以内，资金费率就恰好是 0：币安 TQQQ 上线以来 68% 的结算是 0；周末指数冻结，周末结算
84% 到 85% 是 0，按结算时间算的周末均值约为 0（年化 −0.4%），但非零的周末结算和工作日一样大。这些机制在币安、OKX、Gate、
KuCoin（以及一位核查者看到的 Aster）得到核实；Bitget、MEXC 不公开利率分量，Bybit 没有查到。

**哪一个维持保证金是"真实的"没有定论**，而报告的标题数字取决于它：Gate 给 1%（但资金费率封顶 ±0.02%，指数是代币），
MEXC 和 OKX 给 2%，币安和 Bitget 给 2.5%，KuCoin 5% 到 16.7%。所以本文按维持保证金分档给出结果，不选一个"现实"的值。

**仓位能做多大。** Bitget 第一档（2.5%）对应 1/3 对冲约 30 万美元的账户、0.2 对冲约 50 万；Gate 的 1% 档约 60 万 /
100 万；MEXC 的 2% 约 500 万；KuCoin 只在 5 千 USDT 名义价值以内允许 5 倍（约 1.5 万美元的账户）。没有人检查过持仓量，
也没有人测过 10 万到 100 万美元的空头能否按报价手续费成交。

## 2. 资金费率实际付了多少

- **共同窗口 2026-08-06 到 11-04（三个月）**，年化均值（费率 × 3 × 365，不复利）：Gate 4.6%，币安 4.8%，MEXC 4.8%，
  Bitget 5.9%，OKX 7.5%，KuCoin 5.2%（专题研究者的 6.7% 用的 CSV 少了 105 次结算，补齐后是 5.2%）。币安上线以来 4.0%。
- **按月（币安）**：7 月 2.7%，8 月 −0.09%（整月；从 8 月 6 日起算 −0.06%），9 月 +0.11%，10 月 +0.82%（年化 9.6%）。
  最近 15 天：币安 0.45%，MEXC 0.45%，Bitget 0.39%，OKX 0.82%；11 月头四天年化 30% 到 40%。最近 30 天年化：币安 13.1%，
  MEXC 13.0%，OKX 16.2%，Bitget 9.5%。
- **群里的"半个月 0.5%"**：年化是 12.2%（0.5% × 24 = 12.0%，复利 12.7%），不是 14%。它和最近 15 天相符，但不是上线以来
  最好的两周：币安最好的 15 天是 10 月 9 日到 23 日的 0.685%，最近 15 天在 120 个滚动窗口里排第 14；0.5% 以上的两周只在
  10 月中旬以后出现过，约三周，而四个月的平均水平是每两周 0.16% 到 0.20%。
- **方向没有定论。** README 说"暴跌时资金费率转负"。BTC 永续确实如此：币安 BTC 在 2020 年 3 月 8 到 20 日的崩盘窗口年化
  −21%（整月 −6.3%），FTX 两周（2022-11-07 到 21）−7.9%，但专题研究者的 7 个崩盘窗口里只有这两个为负，BTC 资金费率和
  10 日涨幅的相关系数是 +0.39（涨得越多费率越高）。2026 年的股票永续到目前为止相反：币安 TQQQ 日资金费率和 QQQ 10 日涨幅
  的相关系数 −0.29（83 到 88 个交易日，95% 区间 [−0.45, −0.09]，只有约 9 个独立窗口）；QQQ 跌 3% 到 7% 之后的十天付空头
  年化 16%，涨 3% 到 7% 之后约 −2%；2026 年两次最大的 10 日 TQQQ 下跌（到 7 月 29 日 −22.4%，到 10 月 23 日 −18.4%）
  付了 +5.9% 和 +16.5%。但"急涨时转负"主要靠 2026 年 4 月（币安 QQQ 永续上线当月，均值 −34%）：去掉 4 月，QQQ 永续的相关系数
  只有 −0.05；而到 6 月 10 日的 −15.2% 下跌（币安上线前）KuCoin 年化 −26.6%，正是 README 担心的模式。样本是四到七个月的新
  合约，两种模式都没有确立。
- **只有水平重要，形状几乎无关。** 用研究的模拟器跑 2010 到 2026 年的逐日资金费率路径（常数情形精确复现）：任何重新定中到
  10% 均值的路径（随行情变化、崩盘为负、急涨为负、按 BTC/ETH 形状）把 1/3 对冲的年化最多移动约 0.5 个点。按观察到的常数
  3.8% 和 5.9%，1/3 对冲年化 1.9% 到 3.4%（5 倍 / 3 倍），都低于 3.6% 到 3.7% 的同波动率组合。
- **实际收到的 2026 年窗口**（2026-06-25 到 10-27，87 个交易日，QQQ −0.15%）：用币安实际资金费率（按日历日正确计算）
  1/3 对冲收益 +0.16%（5 倍）/ +0.74%（3 倍），国库券 +1.31%。专题研究者的 +0.34% / +0.90% 把工作日费率填到了周末，多算了
  约 70%。但同一窗口按常数 10% 也只有 +0.84%（5 倍），因为一次爆仓花掉 1.31%，所以这个窗口说明不了资金费率的水平。
- **BTC 的先例不支持"资金费率会逐年衰减"**：币安 BTC 年化 2020 年 17.2%，2021 年 30.6%，2022 年 4.2%，2023 年 7.9%，
  2024 年 11.9%，2025 年 5.1%，2026 年 3.1%，是周期性的，2020 到 2026 年均值 11.6%。

## 3. 爆仓的真实成本（诚实的算法）

- **"损失保证金"不是额外成本。** 模拟器在爆仓时把到爆仓价的价格损失计入"空头盈亏"，把 1% 的维持保证金残余计入"手续费"，
  然后又把整笔被清零的保证金记进"损失保证金"。净值恒等式 净值变化 = 现货收益 + 利息 + 资金费率 + 空头盈亏 − 手续费 不含
  这一项（残差 1e-15）。README 的"每年损失 5% 到 6% 的净值，磨损都花在续杯上"和分年表里的"损失保证金"列（2020 年 −17.0%）
  都是重复计数。表格已改为备注列"Margin wiped (memo)"，不要再相加。
- **爆仓规则的真实成本**：关掉爆仓（无限补仓，不可交易的反事实）后基准从 4.01% 升到 5.02%：5 倍杠杆每年约 1.0 个点，
  3 倍 0.2，2 倍 0.1，10 倍 3.7 到 3.8，0.2 对冲加 5 倍 0.6。现金成本是 1% 的残余（期初净值的 1.48% / 年）加每次爆仓的两笔
  吃单手续费（0.07% / 年）。可交易的比较是表里的 3 倍行：4.6% 对 4.0%，5 倍杠杆多付 0.6 个点。
- **爆仓有多频繁**（1/3 对冲、每月重置、+10%、维持保证金 1%）：5 倍 16.7 年 50 次，分布在 47 个月，每年 3.0 次；静态的
  18.8% 线在 200 个月里被 TQQQ 的月内最高价越过 56 次（28% 的月份，每年 3.4 次；模拟少一些是因为收到的资金费率把线抬到约
  19.6%）；2020 年 8 次，2023 年 6 次；10 个交易日内成对出现的只有 3 次。3 倍的线是 32%，被越过 12 个月（每年 0.7 次）；
  2 倍是 48.5%，16.7 年只有 2 次（2010-09-30、2026-04-24）。数字的口径每次都要说清：3.4 是静态线，3.0 是 +10% 模拟，
  3.54 是资金费率 0，3.2 到 3.3 是 24 小时代理，4.1 到 4.5 是 2.5% 维持保证金。
- **不是插针，是整月的累计涨幅。** 50 次爆仓里，从前一日收盘到爆仓价的中位数只有 2.8%，52%（26 次）当天收盘回到线下，
  48% 收在线上（专题研究者把方向写反了），8 次（16%）开盘就已过线。但"一半是本可以撑到收盘的插针"不成立：这 26 次里有
  23 次按收盘价计算也会在月底重置前爆仓（19 次就在下一个交易日），只有 3 次能撑到重置（2012-08-21、2022-03-29、2023-02-02）。
  杀死仓位的是当月的累计上涨，不是当天的尖峰。
- **维持保证金分档是最大的机械修正。** 用研究自己的模拟器改维持保证金：5 倍在 Bitget 的 2.5% 下，基准从 4.0% 跌到 1.3%
  （比组合少 3.1 个点，每年 4.1 次爆仓）；3 倍从 4.6% 到 4.2%（+0.5）；2 倍 4.5% 到 4.4%。KuCoin 的分档下，10 万美元账户
  1/3 对冲只能用 4 倍（12.5%）：按分档逐日变化模拟年化 −9.9%，最大回撤 −83%（固定在 12.5% 则是 −26%、−99%）；5 倍在任何
  账户规模都亏钱，3 倍从约 3 万美元起亏（1 万美元的账户 3 倍还有 +2.4%，比组合少 2.0）。Gate 的 1% 档：10 万美元账户 5 倍
  复现研究的基准（3.7%，含 0.075% 吃单费，和组合持平），50 万美元账户 2.8%（−1.2）；MEXC 的 2%：5 倍 2.45%（−1.7），
  3 倍 4.6%（+1.0）。
- **24 小时交易的低估不大。** 用纳斯达克 100 期货（NQ=F，约 23 小时，对 TQQQ 的贝塔 2.88，相关 0.977）的最高价乘 3 作为
  全天候代理：5 倍从 3.0 升到 3.3 次 / 年（去掉两次换月假象后 3.2），年化 4.0% 到 3.8%，比组合 +0.3 变成约 0 到 −0.1；
  3 倍不变（0.7）；2 倍从 2 次变 3 次。真实永续的小时 K 线（Bitget 151 个交易日，KuCoin 118 个）：盘外最高价高于当日纽交所
  最高价的占 53% 到 55%，高出 2% 以上只有 25 天，而且其中 21 到 22 天是纽交所跳空低开日（永续还没跌下来）；最大的隔夜
  涨幅是 2026-04-08 的 +11.3%（当日纽交所最高 +10.8%）；周末小时波幅中位数 0.2% 到 0.3%，最大 2.95%（Bitget）/ 6.95%
  （KuCoin）。样本里没有崩盘，代理也不是标记价格。
- **手续费和滑点只在 5 倍重要。** Bitget 公开的强平手续费 0.06% 让 5 倍基准从 4.01% 变 3.95%（+0.25 对组合）；每次续杯
  0.5% 的滑点（假设，未测量）到 3.5%（−0.3）；0.5% 的压力强平费（公开费率的约八倍）到 3.1%，两者一起 2.4%（−1.7）。
  3 倍在同样压力下仍有 4.4% 到 4.5%（比组合多 0.8 以上）。
- **"等下次重置再开"看起来更好（5 倍 5.4% 对 4.0%）只因为 11% 的交易日没有对冲**：波动率 6.7% 对 2.4%，最大回撤 −12.8%
  对 −2.4%，比它自己的同波动率组合少 2.0 个点；逐年差异从 +8.9（2021）到 −10.7（2026，十个月），符号由爆仓后 TQQQ 的走向决定。

  2020 年全年 +4.2%，2023 年 +3.5%。

## 4. "磨损"到底是什么

- **总量复现。** 固定仓位的 TQQQ 空头每月重置，2010-02 到 2026-10 比 −3 倍 QQQ 每年多赚名义价值的 7.40%（代码审计的
  口径 7.35%）；2010 到 2019 年 3.95% 到 3.99%，2020 到 2026 年 12.28% 到 12.34%。
- **怎么拆取决于定义。** README 的"4.8 个点费用和融资 + 2.6 个点复利"来自仓里重建基金的成本模型（`portfolio_lab/leveraged.py`：
  管理费 0.84% + 2 × (国库券 1.49% + 拟合利差 0.51%) = 4.84% / 年），可以复现（4.82 + 2.53）。按真实基金逐日相对 3 倍 QQQ
  的差额算是 5.32 + 2.03；按二阶公式 3(Σr² − R²) 是 4.69 + 2.66；按无成本的日度重置 3 倍基金算是 5.02 + 2.33。所以：
  成本 4.7 到 5.3 个点，复利 2.0 到 2.7 个点。管理费 0.84% 是仓里的常数，ProShares 现在公布的是毛 0.94% / 净 0.78%。
- **复利项不是稳定收入。** 它是每年 12.64% 的日度重置衰减减去 10.29% 的趋势回吐，201 个月里 80 个月为负（41%），
  月均 +0.17%，t 值 1.7：点估计为正但和 0 分不开；去掉 2020 年 3 月降到 +1.5% / 年，去掉最大的三个月降到 +0.8%。
  对带漂移的随机游走，它的期望约 −0.9% / 年。最差的月份是 2026 年 4 月（QQQ +15.7%，TQQQ +52.4%，空头损失名义价值的 5.4%）。
- **持有期越长越少，但不是单调的**：每周重置 +1.45% / 年，每月 +2.35%，每季 −0.44%（按 README 的"总差额减模型成本"口径是
  +0.40%），按日历年 −6.20%。按日历年 TQQQ 在 17 年里有 7 年跑赢 3 倍 QQQ：2013 年多 29.8 个点（TQQQ +139.7% 对 +109.9%），
  2017 年 20.1，2019 年 17.0，2022 年 18.6（下跌趋势也回吐：QQQ −32.6%，TQQQ −79.1% 对 −97.7%），2023 年 33.5。
- **2020 到 2026 年磨损变大主要是利率，不是震荡。** 两个时期差 8.3 个点：5.7 个点是费用和融资项（国库券从 0.56% 升到 2.85%，
  基金借两个单位：2 × 升幅 = 4.6 个点；其余 1.1 是跟踪残差），2.7 个点是净复利项（衰减多了 9.4 个点，但趋势回吐也多了 6.7；
  QQQ 波动率 17% 对 25%）。2023 年每月口径的差额 +9.3% 全部是成本项（13.9 = 2 × 5.04% 国库券 10.1 + 费用利差 1.9 +
  跟踪 2.0），复利项是 −4.7%。
- **对 1/3 对冲意味着什么。** 空头名义价值在重置时约为净值的 0.30，收到的费用加融资约为净值的 1.4% 到 1.6% / 年，
  和同样资金买国库券（1.5%）差不多；而比较用的组合除了 87% 到 88% 的国库券还有 12% 到 13% 的 QQQ，后者贡献了它 3.7% 到
  3.9% 年化里的 2.2 到 2.5 个点。所以没有资金费率时，全额对冲在手续费和爆仓之前就没有优势。
- **VIX 不是开关。** 按月初 VIX 分组，空头相对 −3 倍的收益 VIX<15 时 +6.7% / 年，15 到 20 +6.6%，20 到 30 +4.2%
  （复利项 −0.4，56% 的月份跑赢；t 值 0.95，和其他组分不开），≥30 +26.4%（13 个月，其中 2020 年 3 月一个月贡献 16.3%，
  去掉是 +11.4%）。按季度 20 到 30 组 −6.6% / 年（12 个季度，两个季度决定，去掉两者 +1.4%；季度起点改为 2、5、8、11 月
  则 +10.7%）。按期内平均 VIX 分组，20 到 30 反而是季度最好的组（+8.8%）。结论只有"月初 VIX 预测不了磨损"。
- **盈亏平衡资金费率**（相对同波动率组合，2010 到 2026 年）：1/3 对冲 5 倍 9.0%，3 倍 6.5%，2 倍 6.4%；0.2 对冲 5 倍 5.9%，
  3 倍 2.8%，2 倍 2.7%。分时期：2010 到 2019 年 7.3% / 5.2%（1/3 对冲 5 倍 / 3 倍），2020 到 2026 年 10.8% / 7.3%。
  逐年从 −14.2%（2022 年，付资金费率也赢）和 −9.6%（2011）到 +23.5%（2020）和 +27.5%（2023），和当年 QQQ 涨幅的相关系数
  +0.83 到 +0.84，和波动率 −0.08（但控制涨幅后 5 倍的偏相关 +0.34：波动通过爆仓抬高平衡点；2 到 3 倍约 0）。决定平衡点的
  是趋势，这在 2 到 3 倍成立，5 倍还要加上爆仓。

来源：`deep_dive/decay_regimes/`（`decay_decomposition.py`、`breakeven_funding.py`、`cuts.csv`），`deep_dive/code_audit/rederive.py`，
`_verify/decay_regimes-*/`、`_verify/corrections-decay_regimes/`。

## 8. 缺点

- **资金费率是参数，不是观察。** +10% 高于所有交易所三个月的均值（4.6% 到 7.5%），只有最近 30 天达到；0.5% 的半个月是
  12.2% 年化，且只出现过约三周。方向（崩盘时转负还是转正）没有定论（第 2 节）。
- **监管内的替代品没有比较过。** 做空纳斯达克 100 期货（MNQ / NQ）给同样的贝塔对冲，没有保证金清零，隐含融资约为国库券加
  0.3% 到 0.5%，美国 60/40 税收待遇；永续相对它的优势是资金费率减（国库券加利差）减爆仓成本，在 5% 费率、4.4% 利率下约为 0。
  研究只和少拿现货、融券做空、SQQQ 比过。
- **自动减仓和下架。** 崩盘时空头是最赚钱的仓位，保险基金不足时最先被自动减仓强平（KuCoin 和币安的合约都标注 `isDeleverage: true`），
  也就是对冲恰好在起作用时可能被拿走；下架则按交易所的指数强制现金交割，时间由不得你。没有人建模或估算。Aster 93% 的结算为 0
  说明小交易所盘口很薄。
- **税务和会计。** 美国持有人：境外加密永续不是 §1256 合约，多 QQQ 空 TQQQ 永续很可能构成 §1092 跨式（亏损递延，持有期重置），
  收到的资金费率是普通收入；中国居民：加密衍生品交易本身是问题。回测不变，但足以抹掉 0 到 1 个点的优势。
- **基差。** Bitget 永续在美东 16:00 平均比纽交所收盘价高 0.32%（标准差 0.15%，最大 +0.76%，150 天），KuCoin 高 0.25%，
  从未相差 1% 以上；周五收盘到周一开盘的永续变动（标准差 2.5%，−5% 到 +6.5%）和 TQQQ 自己的跳空一样大。
- **仓位大小和流动性**（第 1 节）：分档随名义价值上升，持仓量和实际滑点没有人测。
- **分红。** TQQQ 除息（收益率约 1%）和各交易所指数是否调整没有查；方向已知（指数不调整时空头赚除息跌幅），金额小。
- **样本内选择。** 0.2 / 2 倍配方是在同一段 2010 到 2026 年样本上选出并"确认"的；55 个滚动窗口约 5 段独立；同波动率组合事后配置。
- **原报告已有的**：合约在美股休市时交易（第 3 节已量化，样本无崩盘）；不重置时对冲比例漂移（涨时上升、跌后塌缩：
  "从不重置"平均 0.31，2022 到 2023 年只有 0.17 到 0.19，它多出的 2.7 个点约 1.1 来自更低的平均对冲，其余来自跌后反弹
  期间对冲不足、一半的手续费和一半的爆仓）；交易所对手方风险；对冲掉的部分等于不持有现货。

# English version

## The answer in five lines

1. **At the margin and funding the venues actually show** (2.5% maintenance, as at Binance and Bitget, and funding of about
   +5% a year), the one-third hedge earns about what T-bills pay (2.6-2.9% a year over 2010-2026) and trails simply holding
   less QQQ (the same-volatility QQQ/T-bill mix) by 0.6-1.1 points a year, and by 2-3 points over the last three years.
   The study's base case (one-third, 5x) loses money there: -0.7% a year, 5.1 points below the mix, liquidated 4.5 times a year.
2. **The one recipe that is not clearly worse than holding less QQQ**: hedge 0.20 of spot value in TQQQ notional, 2x isolated
   margin, reset monthly. At 2.5% maintenance and +5% funding it is +0.3 points above the same-volatility mix over 2010-2026
   and -0.5 below it over the last three years, a wash; it beats the mix by about 1.3 points only if funding stays near +10%,
   which it has done only since mid-October 2026.
3. **Every point of edge is funding.** The fund's "decay" (7.4% a year of TQQQ notional over a plain -3x, held in fixed units for
   a month) is mostly the fund's own fee and financing (4.7-5.3 points), which on a one-third hedge collects about what T-bills
   pay on the same capital; the rest (2.0-2.7 points) is a bet that the index chops, negative in 80 of 201 months. Liquidations
   at 5x cost about 1 point a year, not the "5-6% of NAV" the first write-up said.
4. **If you still want it**: hedge 0.20 (or 0.25) of spot value in TQQQ notional, 2x isolated margin, monthly reset, on a venue
   whose maintenance is 2.5% or lower for your size (Binance, Bitget up to 100k USDT, MEXC 2%, OKX 2%, Gate 1%). TQQQ must rise
   about 46-48% from the last reset price within a month to liquidate it (twice in 16.7 years) against 17-19% at 5x (three to
   four times a year). Never 5x on KuCoin's tiers, where a $100k book is capped at 4x and lost 9.9% a year.
5. **Expect T-bills plus whatever funding pays above about 3%** (the 0.20 / 2x break-even over 2010-2026; 6-7% at today's rates),
   and accept that $1 in February 2010 became $4.72 against $18.89 in QQQ. The exchange, auto-deleveraging, delisting, tax and
   custody questions are unsized by any backtest and can erase an edge of under a point.

**Funding mechanics.** Binance's formula is funding = premium index + clamp(interest - premium index, ±0.05%). For stock
perpetuals the interest component is set to 0, so funding is exactly zero whenever the 8-hour average premium is within ±0.05%:
68% of Binance's TQQQ settlements since listing were zero. The index is frozen at weekends; 84-85% of weekend settlements were
zero and the weekend mean by settlement time is about zero (-0.4% annualised), but the non-zero weekend prints are as large as
weekday ones. Interest 0 is verified on Binance, OKX, Gate and KuCoin (and Aster per one verifier); Bitget and MEXC do not expose
it and Bybit was not reached.

**Which maintenance level is "real" is unresolved**, and the headline depends on it: Gate offers 1% (but with funding capped at
±0.02% and a token index), MEXC and OKX 2%, Binance and Bitget 2.5%, KuCoin 5-16.7%. This report therefore gives results by
maintenance level rather than picking one "realistic" value.

**Position size.** Bitget's tier 1 (2.5%) covers a book of about $300k at hedge 1/3 or $500k at 0.2; Gate's 1% tier about
$600k / $1M; MEXC's 2% about $5M; KuCoin allows 5x only up to 5,000 USDT notional (a $15k book). Nobody checked open interest
or whether a $100k-1M short is executable at the quoted fees.

  funding was -21% annualised over the 8-20 March 2020 crash window (-6.3% for the whole month) and -7.9% over the FTX fortnight

## 3. What liquidation really costs (the honest measure)

- **"Margin lost" is not an extra cost.** At a liquidation the simulator books the price loss to the liquidation level in
  "short P&L", the 1% maintenance remnant in "fees", and then adds the whole wiped margin to "margin lost" again. The NAV identity
  NAV change = spot gain + interest + funding + short P&L - fees holds without it (residual 1e-15). The README's "losing 5-6% of
  NAV a year in margin, so the decay was spent on refills" and the year table's "margin lost" column (-17.0% in 2020) were double
  counts. The tables now carry it as a memo column, "Margin wiped (memo)", not to be added.
- **What the liquidation rule really costs**: with liquidations switched off (unlimited top-up, not a tradable position) the base
  case goes from 4.01% to 5.02%: about 1.0 point a year at 5x, 0.2 at 3x, 0.1 at 2x, 3.7-3.8 at 10x, 0.6 for hedge 0.2 at 5x.
  The cash cost is the 1% remnant (1.48% of starting NAV a year) plus two taker fees per liquidation (0.07% a year). The tradable
  comparison is the 3x row of the tables: 4.6% against 4.0%, a 0.6-point penalty for 5x.
- **How often** (hedge 1/3, monthly, +10%, maintenance 1%): 5x was liquidated 50 times in 16.7 years, in 47 distinct months, 3.0 a
  year. TQQQ's intraday high crossed the static 18.8% line above the prior month-end close in 56 of 200 months (28% of months,
  3.4 a year; the simulation shows fewer because funding received lifts the line to about 19.6%); 8 times in 2020 and 6 in 2023;
  only 3 pairs within 10 trading days. At 3x the line is 32%, crossed in 12 months (0.7 a year); at 2x 48.5%, crossed twice in
  16.7 years (2010-09-30, 2026-04-24). Name the convention each time: 3.4 is the static line, 3.0 the +10% simulation, 3.54 at
  zero funding, 3.2-3.3 the 24/7 proxy, 4.1-4.5 at 2.5% maintenance.
- **Not wicks: the month's cumulative rise.** Of the 50 liquidations the median move from the prior close to the liquidation
  price was 2.8%; 52% (26) closed back below the line, 48% closed above it (the specialist had the direction reversed), and 8
  (16%) were already past it at the open. But "half are wicks the position would have survived at the close" fails: 23 of those
  26 would have been liquidated anyway before the monthly reset if merely marked at the close (19 on the very next day); only 3
  of 50 would have reached the reset (2012-08-21, 2022-03-29, 2023-02-02). The month's cumulative rise, not the day's spike,
  finishes the position.
- **The maintenance tier is the largest mechanical correction.** Re-run with the study's own simulator: at Bitget's 2.5% the 5x
  base case falls from 4.0% to 1.3% a year (3.1 points below the mix, 4.1 liquidations a year); 3x goes 4.6% to 4.2% (+0.5 vs mix);
  2x 4.5% to 4.4%. On KuCoin's tiers a $100k book hedged 1/3 can only use 4x (12.5%): simulated with the tier falling as the book
  shrinks, -9.9% a year and a -83% worst fall (at a fixed 12.5%, -26% a year and -99%); 5x loses money at every book size, 3x from
  about a $30k book (a $10k book still earns +2.4% at 3x, 2.0 below the mix). On Gate's 1% tier a $100k book at 5x reproduces the
  study's base case (3.7%, with Gate's 0.075% taker fee in the trigger, level with the mix); a $500k book 2.8% (-1.2). On MEXC's 2%:
  5x 2.45% (-1.7), 3x 4.6% (+1.0).
- **The 24/7 understatement is small.** With the Nasdaq-100 futures' ~23-hour high (NQ=F, beta 2.88 to TQQQ, correlation 0.977)
  scaled 3x as a 24/7 proxy, 5x goes from 3.0 to 3.3 liquidations a year (3.2 after removing two quarterly-roll artefacts), CAGR
  4.0% to 3.8%, vs mix +0.3 to about 0 / -0.1; 3x is unchanged (0.7); 2x goes from 2 events to 3. The real perpetuals' hourly
  candles (Bitget 151 NYSE days from 2026-03-31, KuCoin 118 from 2026-05-18) show the off-hours high above the day's NYSE high on
  53-55% of days, by more than 2% on only 25 days, of which 21-22 are NYSE gap-down days where the perp simply had not fallen yet;
  the largest overnight move was +11.3% on 2026-04-08 (NYSE high +10.8%); weekend hourly ranges median 0.2-0.3%, max 2.95%
  (Bitget) / 6.95% (KuCoin). There is no crash in the sample and the proxy is not a mark price.
- **Fees and slippage matter at 5x only.** Bitget's documented 0.06% close-out fee moves the 5x base from 4.01% to 3.95% (+0.25
  vs mix); 0.5% slippage on each refill (assumed, not measured) to 3.5% (-0.3); a stress close-out fee of 0.5% (about eight times
  the documented rate) to 3.1%; both together 2.4% (-1.7 vs mix). At 3x the same stresses leave 4.4-4.5% (still +0.8 or more vs mix).
- **"Wait for the next reset" looks better (5x: 5.4% vs 4.0%) only because it is unhedged 11% of trading days**: volatility 6.7% vs
  2.4%, worst fall -12.8% vs -2.4%, and 2.0 points below its own same-volatility mix; the year-by-year refill-minus-wait difference
  swings from +8.9 points (2021) to -10.7 (2026, ten months), the sign set by where TQQQ went after the liquidation.
- **2020's 8 liquidations** wiped margin summing to 16.3% of the previous day's NAV, but that is gross (each balance contains the
  short loss the spot leg offset); the hedged portfolio made +4.2% in 2020 and +3.5% in 2023.

## 4. What the "decay" really is

- **The total reproduces.** A fixed-unit TQQQ short reset monthly earned 7.40% a year of notional more than a plain -3x over
  2010-02 to 2026-10 (7.35% under the code audit's convention); 3.95-3.99% in 2010-2019, 12.28-12.34% in 2020-2026.
- **The split depends on the definition.** The README's "4.8 points fee and financing + 2.6 compounding" comes from the repo's
  rebuilt fund's cost model (`portfolio_lab/leveraged.py`: expense 0.84% + 2 x (T-bill 1.49% + fitted spread 0.51%) = 4.84% a year)
  and reproduces (4.82 + 2.53). Measured day by day on the real fund against 3x QQQ it is 5.32 + 2.03; with the second-order
  formula 3(sum r² - R²) it is 4.69 + 2.66; against a costless daily-reset 3x fund 5.02 + 2.33. So: cost 4.7-5.3 points,
  compounding 2.0-2.7 points. The 0.84% expense is the repo's constant; ProShares now shows 0.94% gross / 0.78% net.
- **The compounding term is not an income.** It is a 12.64% a year daily-reset decay minus a 10.29% a year trend give-back,
  negative in 80 of 201 months (41%), monthly mean +0.17%, t = 1.7: a positive point estimate not distinguishable from zero. It
  drops to +1.5% a year without March 2020 and to +0.8% without the three largest months. For a random walk with QQQ's drift its
  expectation is about -0.9% a year. The worst month was April 2026 (QQQ +15.7%, TQQQ +52.4%, the short gave up 5.4% of notional).
- **It shrinks with the holding period, but not monotonically**: +1.45% a year with weekly resets, +2.35% monthly, -0.44% quarterly
  (+0.40% under the README's "gap minus model cost" convention), -6.20% over calendar years. By calendar year TQQQ beat three times
  QQQ in 7 of 17 years: 2013 by 29.8 points (TQQQ +139.7% vs 3xQQQ +109.9%), 2017 by 20.1, 2019 by 17.0, 2022 by 18.6 (a down
  trend gives back too: QQQ -32.6%, TQQQ -79.1% vs -97.7%), 2023 by 33.5.
- **The bigger 2020-2026 decay is mostly interest rates, not choppier markets.** Of the 8.3-point difference between the eras,
  5.7 points is the fee-and-financing term (T-bills went from 0.56% to 2.85% and the fund borrows two units: 2 x the rise = 4.6
  points; the other 1.1 is tracking residual) and 2.7 points the net compounding term (the variance decay rose 9.4 points but the
  trend give-back rose 6.7; QQQ volatility 17% vs 25%). In 2023 the monthly gap of +9.3% was entirely the cost term (13.9 = 2 x
  the 5.04% T-bill 10.1 + fee and spread 1.9 + tracking 2.0) with a compounding term of -4.7%.
- **What it means for a one-third hedge.** Short notional at a reset is about 0.30 of NAV, so the fee and financing collected are
  about 1.4-1.6% of NAV a year, roughly what T-bills paid on the same capital (1.5%); and the comparison mix holds 12-13% QQQ
  besides 87-88% T-bills, which contributed 2.2-2.5 of its 3.7-3.9% a year. Without funding the full hedge has no edge before fees
  and liquidations.
- **VIX is not a switch.** By VIX at the start of the month the short's gain over -3x was +6.7% a year (VIX < 15), +6.6% (15-20),
  +4.2% (20-30; compounding term -0.4, short beat -3x in 56% of months; t = 0.95, indistinguishable from the other bands), +26.4%
  (>= 30; 13 months, of which March 2020 alone contributed 16.3%, +11.4% without it). Held quarterly the 20-30 band lost 6.6% a
  year (12 quarters, driven by two; +1.4% without them; +10.7% with quarters starting Feb/May/Aug/Nov). Sorted by the period's mean
  VIX instead, 20-30 is the best quarterly band (+8.8%). The only robust statement is that VIX at the start of a period does not
  predict the gain.
- **Break-even funding** against the same-volatility mix, 2010-2026: one-third hedge at 5x 9.0%, 3x 6.5%, 2x 6.4%; hedge 0.2 at
  5x 5.9%, 3x 2.8%, 2x 2.7%. By era 7.3% / 5.2% (2010-2019, one-third at 5x / 3x) and 10.8% / 7.3% (2020-2026). Year by year
  from -14.2% (2022, the hedge wins even paying funding) and -9.6% (2011) to +23.5% (2020) and +27.5% (2023); correlation with the
  year's QQQ return +0.83 to +0.84, with realised volatility -0.08 (but the partial correlation with volatility, controlling for the
  return, is +0.34 at 5x, through liquidations; about 0 at 2-3x). The trend sets the break-even at 2-3x; at 5x liquidations add to it.

Sources: `deep_dive/decay_regimes/` (`decay_decomposition.py`, `breakeven_funding.py`, `cuts.csv`), `deep_dive/code_audit/rederive.py`,
`_verify/decay_regimes-*/`, `_verify/corrections-decay_regimes/`.

## 8. The drawbacks

- **Funding is a parameter, not an observation.** +10% is above every venue's three-month mean (4.6-7.5%) and was reached only in the
  last 30 days; 0.5% per half month is 12.2% a year and has occurred for about three weeks. Its direction in a crash is not
  established either way (section 2).
- **The regulated alternative was never compared.** Shorting Nasdaq-100 futures (MNQ / NQ) gives the same beta hedge with no margin
  wipe, implied financing near T-bill + 0.3-0.5% and US 60/40 tax treatment; the perp's edge over it is funding minus (T-bill +
  spread) minus liquidation costs, roughly zero at 5% funding and 4.4% rates. The study compared only holding less, a stock-loan
  short and SQQQ.
- **Auto-deleveraging and delisting.** In a crash the short is the most profitable position and the first to be force-closed by ADL
  when the insurance fund runs short (KuCoin's and Binance's contracts carry `isDeleverage: true`): the hedge can be removed exactly
  when it works. A delisting forces cash settlement at the venue's index, with no say on timing. Nobody modelled or sized either;
  Aster's 93% zero settlements show how thin the smaller books are.
- **Tax and accounting.** For a US holder, offshore crypto perpetuals are not §1256 contracts, long QQQ against a short TQQQ perp is
  likely a §1092 straddle (losses deferred, holding period reset), and funding received is ordinary income; for a PRC-resident holder,
  crypto-derivative trading itself is the question. None of it changes the backtest, but it can erase a 0-1 point edge.
- **Basis.** At 16:00 ET the Bitget perp closed on average 0.32% above the NYSE close (sd 0.15%, max +0.76%, 150 days) and KuCoin
  0.25%, never more than 1% apart; the Friday-close-to-Monday-open perp move (sd 2.5%, -5% to +6.5%) is the same as TQQQ's own gap.
- **Size and liquidity** (section 1): tiers rise with notional; open interest and real slippage were not measured.
- **Dividends.** TQQQ's ex-dividend drops (yield about 1%) and whether each venue's index adjusts for them were not examined; the
  sign is known (the short gains the drop on an unadjusted index), the size is small.
- **In-sample selection.** The 0.2 / 2x recipe was chosen on the same 2010-2026 sample it is "confirmed" on; the 55 rolling windows
  hold about five independent periods; the same-volatility mix is set with hindsight.
- **Already in the first write-up**: the contract trades when the stock market is closed (sized in section 3, no crash in the
  sample); without resets the hedge ratio drifts (it rises in rallies and collapses after falls: "reset never" held 0.31 on average
  but 0.17-0.19 through 2022-2023, and of its extra 2.7 points about 1.1 is the lower average hedge, the rest being under-hedged
  through the 2023 rebound, half the fees and half the liquidations); exchange counterparty risk; the hedged part is spot you do not own.

## 10. What is still unknown

- Which maintenance level applies to you: it depends on the venue, the notional tier, and whether Gate's token index and ±0.02%
  funding cap are acceptable; Bybit's tiers were not read.
- Stock-perpetual funding in a crash: no sample. How the mark price behaves in a closed-hours crash: only Bitget's rule text (index
  at most 10% from the last close, clamp "typically 3%", TQQQ not listed).
- The probability and cost of auto-deleveraging and delisting; real slippage and open interest for a $100k-1M short; refill
  slippage in a spike (0.5% assumed; the hourly candles the specialists held were not used to measure it).
- Tax (US straddle rules, the character of funding income; compliance for PRC residents) and USDT / exchange custody risk on
  10-20% of NAV.
- Dividends and each venue's index adjustment.
- The long-run funding level: four months to a year of new contracts; BTC's precedent is cyclical and does not extrapolate.
- Out of sample: the recipes were chosen on the sample they are tested on; one 2010-2026 history, mostly a bull market; 1999-2010
  only with a rebuilt fund and liquidations on the close.
