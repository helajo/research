Why Sell

1

A DCF built on WD's own model shows material downside — even in the Bull Case

Discounting our modeled FY27E–FY31E unlevered FCF at a Blume-adjusted 13.9% WACC gives a Base Case DCF of $160.01/share. Even the Bull Case — 18% five-year revenue CAGR, the Seagate HAMR gap closed, a 44% terminal EBITDA margin — tops out at $256, still 51% below the tape.

2

No reasonable assumption grid gets there

Sensitizing WACC 11.0%–17.0% and terminal growth 1.5%–3.5% never breaks $194. Sensitizing revenue CAGR 8%–24% and terminal EBITDA margin 30%–46% — a corner more aggressive than our own Bull Case — tops out at $358. Every cell in both grids sits below the current price.

3

History says every HDD upcycle ends the same way

WD posted a $1.7bn net loss in FY2023, barely two years before this supercycle began. The capacity response is already underway on both sides: WD's own HAMR ramp (1H CY2027 volume target) and Seagate's Mozaic 4+ platform (already shipping) are explicitly designed to close the current supply gap that is driving today's 50%+ gross margin.

4

The Street is uniformly bullish and still raising targets

22 of 26 analysts rate it Buy/Strong Buy, avg. target $633.83 — above the tape — and every one of the last 15 rating actions we reviewed was a target increase. That pattern looks more like chasing a fast-moving momentum stock than settled independent conviction.

Reverse sensitivity check

The revenue CAGR / terminal margin combination required to justify $519.80 sits beyond the top-right corner of our sensitivity grid — 24% sustained five-year CAGR and a 46% terminal EBITDA margin still only reach $358. Read correctly: the market is pricing in a growth runway longer than even our explicitly aggressive Bull Case, not a cycle that ever normalizes.

Risk / Catalyst Map

Biggest risks to the SELL

  • AI demand share keeps climbing past 2030. Task 1 research shows AI-attributable HDD demand at 18% of 2026 shipments, rising to 58% by 2030 — our 5-year window may simply be too short.
  • HAMR closes the Seagate gap early. Qualification ahead of the 1H CY2027 target would undercut the single biggest company-specific risk in our thesis.
  • More multi-year hyperscaler contracts land. FY29E–31E is currently uncontracted in our model; new disclosures would extend high-confidence visibility.
  • Capital return accelerates faster than modeled. Net cash position plus compounding FCF gives real headroom beyond our conservative buyback assumptions.
  • The Street stays right. 22 of 26 analysts Buy/Strong Buy, avg. target $633.83, raised virtually every month this cycle.

Watch next

  • Aug 5, 2026 — FQ4 2026 earnings. First read on whether "sold out through 2026" holds and Q4 consensus ($3.70bn rev, ~$3.30 EPS) is met.
  • HAMR qualification updates. Seagate's Mozaic 4+ is already shipping 44TB drives; any WD timeline slip vs. its 1H CY2027 target matters directly.
  • New multi-year contract disclosures. Any extension into FY29E–31E is the single biggest swing factor for our target.
  • Hyperscaler capex commentary. Amazon/Microsoft/Google/Meta earnings calls — the ultimate demand signal for WD's Cloud segment.
  • Blended $/TB pricing trend. Currently +9% YoY; any deceleration would front-run the margin-normalization already in our model.
