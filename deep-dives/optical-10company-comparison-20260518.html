The Copper Wall thesis in one paragraph: Every generation of NVIDIA GPU roughly doubles bandwidth requirements. Copper cables — the current standard for connecting GPUs, switches, and servers — generate too much heat, consume too much power, and lose signal integrity above ~40 metres at speeds beyond 400G. The industry is in a mandatory, non-optional transition from copper to light. The 10 companies in this report collectively own the photon supply chain that makes AI at scale physically possible.
核心命题：每一代英伟达GPU所需带宽约翻番，铜缆在400G以上速率和40米以上距离时存在严重的热损耗和信号衰减问题。整个行业正在经历从铜到光的强制性、不可逆转的转型。本报告覆盖的10家公司共同拥有这一光子供应链，是AI规模化运行的物理基础。

中文摘要
第2层（算力血管）内部细分为6个子层：2A衬底/晶圆材料（COHR、LITE、GFS、TSEM，也涉及第1层）→2B激光芯片制造（LITE主导，COHR次之，MTSI参与）→2C信号处理硅芯片（MRVL主导DSP，MTSI推进LPO，AVGO参与）→2D收发器模块组装（COHR、LITE、CIEN，FN为合同制造商）→2E光学网络系统（CIEN领导，LITE推OCS，COHR和AVGO参与）→2F物理光学基础设施（GLW主导光纤电缆，GFS为硅光子代工厂）。理解每家公司在价值链中的位置是评估其竞争优势和风险的关键。

SECTION 5 · 第五节
Market Impact of Global Happenings
全球宏观事件的市场影响
🤖 AI Capex Supercycle
Hyperscalers are committing $700B+ in AI capex in 2026 alone. Every dollar spent on GPU clusters requires proportional optical networking investment. This is the core demand driver for all 10 companies. Direction: ↑ for all, but especially LITE, COHR, CIEN, GLW.
AI资本支出超级周期：超大云厂商2026年仅AI资本支出即逾7000亿美元，每一美元GPU集群投资都需要相应的光学网络支出。对组合内所有10家公司均为正向驱动，尤其利好LITE、COHR、CIEN、GLW。
🇺🇸🇨🇳 US-China Trade & Export Controls
China export restrictions on rare earth minerals (gallium, germanium — key InP inputs) suspended until Nov 2026 via trade deal. If resumed: threatens InP laser production (LITE, COHR). US export restrictions on photonics equipment to China: limits COHR/LITE China revenue. US CHIPS Act: favours GFS, TSEM US operations. Net: bifurcated impact.
美中贸易：中国稀土（镓、锗——InP激光器关键原材料）出口管制已暂停至2026年11月，若恢复将威胁LITE、COHR的InP激光芯片生产。美国对华光子设备出口限制压制COHR/LITE中国营收。CHIPS法案利好GFS和TSEM在美扩产。整体影响：分化，需个股分析。
🌐 Geopolitical Risk — Israel (TSEM)
Tower Semiconductor is headquartered in Israel (Migdal Haemek). Ongoing regional tensions create a geopolitical risk discount on TSEM that is not present for US/Thailand-headquartered peers. Any escalation in the Middle East could trigger manufacturing disruption and significant stock volatility. This is the single largest company-specific macro risk in the 10-company group.
以色列地缘政治风险：TSEM总部位于以色列，是组合内唯一具有重大地缘政治风险敞口的公司，中东地区紧张局势升级可能导致制造中断和股价剧烈波动，是10家公司中最大的单一宏观尾部风险。
💰 Interest Rate Environment
Higher-for-longer rates disproportionately impact loss-making high-growth names (COHR's GAAP losses, LITE's transitioning profitability) by increasing the discount rate applied to future earnings. AVGO, GLW, and MRVL — all currently GAAP profitable with dividends/buybacks — are more resilient to rate headwinds. A rate cut cycle would be a meaningful re-rating catalyst for COHR and TSEM specifically.
利率环境：持续高利率对GAAP亏损的高成长公司（COHR、CIEN历史上）影响更大，因折现率提升压低远期盈利的现值。AVGO、GLW、MRVL和GFS均为GAAP盈利且有股息回购，对利率更具韧性。降息周期将对COHR和TSEM产生明显的估值催化效应。
🚗 EV Industry Slowdown (COHR, TSEM)
EV adoption decelerated globally in 2024–2025. Coherent's SiC substrate business and TSEM's automotive process revenue both faced headwinds. However, EV recovery in 2026 (China stimulus, US IRA incentives) is beginning to provide a tailwind. This is a meaningful secondary growth vector that could accelerate earnings ahead of consensus for both.
电动车行业周期：2024-2025年EV增速放缓导致COHR碳化硅衬底和TSEM汽车工艺收入承压。但2026年EV复苏（中国政策刺激、美国IRA激励）逐步提供顺风，可能使两家公司盈利超出市场预期。这是AI光学主题之外的重要次级增长向量。
🔄 DeepSeek-Style Model Efficiency Risk
A breakthrough in AI model efficiency (reducing compute needed for given performance) could temporarily slow hyperscaler GPU orders and optical capex. All 10 companies are correlated to this risk. However, historical precedent (transistor density improvements) shows that efficiency gains tend to unlock new applications, expanding total AI demand rather than reducing it. Management teams universally cite this as a "when, not if" risk but discount long-term impact.
AI模型效率突破风险：DeepSeek式效率提升可能短期放缓超大云厂商GPU采购和光学资本开支，对组合内所有10家公司均有影响。但历史先例（晶体管密度提升）表明，效率提升往往带来新应用扩展，而非减少总需求。各公司管理层普遍将其视为"何时而非是否"的风险，但不认为影响长期趋势。
🏭 US Manufacturing Reshoring
The US CHIPS Act and "America First" trade policy are actively incentivising domestic semiconductor manufacturing. GFS ($1.5B CHIPS Act), TSEM (US fabs), COHR (NVIDIA partnership explicitly tied to US manufacturing expansion), and GLW (NVIDIA partnership centered on US optical capacity) all directly benefit. This creates a geopolitical floor on these companies' strategic value that transcends normal valuation metrics.
美国制造业回流：CHIPS法案和"美国优先"政策为GFS（15亿美元联邦支持）、TSEM（美国晶圆厂）、COHR（英伟达合作明确绑定美国本土制造扩张）和GLW（英伟达合作聚焦美国光学产能）提供直接利好，创造了超越正常估值指标的地缘政治底部支撑。
📡 Telecom 5G/6G Upgrade Cycle
Ciena (CIEN) and MACOM (MTSI) both have significant telecom exposure (~30-40% of revenue each). Global 5G densification and early 6G planning are driving optical networking infrastructure upgrades independent of AI datacenter demand. This provides revenue diversification and a non-AI growth vector for these two companies specifically.
电信5G/6G升级周期：CIEN和MTSI均有约30-40%的电信营收敞口，全球5G致密化和早期6G规划推动光网络基础设施独立于AI数据中心需求的升级。为这两家公司提供非AI增长向量和营收多样化。
🌊 Optical Group Correlation Risk
The entire optical group trades as one correlated basket. When macro risk-off hits, all 10 fall simultaneously and sharply (beta 1.0–2.25 across the group). The positive: this creates identical entry windows across different risk profiles within the same trade. The negative: diversifying across all 10 does NOT reduce sector-level drawdown risk — only adding non-optical positions achieves true diversification.
光学板块整体相关性风险：组合内10家公司作为一个相关性篮子交易，宏观风险事件发生时同步大幅下跌（Beta范围1.0-2.25）。积极面：提供在同一主题内跨不同风险特征同时建仓的机会。消极面：在10家公司内部分散并不能真正降低板块整体回撤风险——只有加入非光学持仓才能实现真正的多样化。
