Concept 01 · 概念一
Normal computers vs. quantum computers
普通电脑 vs 量子电脑
The analogy · 类比
Imagine a maze 🌀
A normal computer is like someone walking through a maze — it tries one path at a time, backs up when stuck, tries another. It's fast, but still one path at a time.

A quantum computer is like flooding the maze with water all at once — the water finds every path simultaneously, and the solution (the exit) is where the water flows out first.
Regular computers store everything as bits — tiny switches that are either OFF (0) or ON (1). Every calculation is a sequence of these tiny switches flipping. They're incredibly fast, but they're still going through options one at a time.
Quantum computers use qubits — and qubits can be 0, 1, or both at the same time (more on this below). This lets them explore enormous numbers of possibilities simultaneously for certain types of hard problems.
中文摘要
想象一个迷宫：普通电脑像一个人在迷宫里一条路一条路地试，试错、退回、再试；量子电脑则像往迷宫里注水——水同时涌入所有通道，出口最快流出的就是答案。普通电脑用"比特（bits）"存储信息，每个比特非0即1；量子电脑用量子比特（qubits），可以同时是0和1，从而同时探索海量可能性。
Concept 02 · 概念二
Superposition — being two things at once
叠加态——同时存在于两种状态
The Spinning Coin Analogy · 旋转硬币类比
H
Classical
Heads (1)
T
Classical
Tails (0)
🌀
Quantum Qubit
Both at once!
A classical bit is like a coin lying flat — either heads or tails. A qubit is like a coin spinning in the air — it's both heads AND tails until you look at it (measure it), at which point it "collapses" to one.
This is called superposition. A qubit isn't just faster than a bit — it's fundamentally different. It carries both possibilities simultaneously until you measure it. The magic is in doing calculations while it's still spinning — before it collapses into a definite answer.
With 2 qubits in superposition, you can represent 4 states at once (00, 01, 10, 11). With 3 qubits: 8 states. With 300 qubits: more states than there are atoms in the observable universe. That's the power of quantum parallelism.
How many states can N qubits represent simultaneously? (2ᴺ)
Each qubit doubles the number of simultaneous states. At ~300 qubits, the number exceeds atoms in the observable universe (~10⁸⁰).
中文摘要
叠加态（Superposition）就像一枚旋转中的硬币——它同时既是正面又是反面，直到你去"看"它（测量），才会"坍缩"成一个确定的结果。量子比特在被测量前同时代表多种可能性，这让量子计算机可以并行处理海量信息。2个量子比特 = 同时表示4种状态；10个 = 1024种；300个 = 比宇宙中所有原子还多的状态数。这就是量子并行的威力所在。
Concept 03 · 概念三
Entanglement — spooky action at a distance
量子纠缠——神秘的"心灵感应"
Einstein called it "spooky" · 爱因斯坦称之为"鬼魅般的远程效应"
Magic gloves 🧤🧤
Imagine you have a pair of gloves. You put one in a box and ship it to Tokyo. When you open your box in Singapore and see a left glove — you instantly know Tokyo has the right glove.

Quantum entanglement is like that, but weirder: neither glove decided to be left or right until you opened the box. The act of measuring one instantly determines the other — no matter how far apart they are.
Entanglement links two qubits together so that the state of one instantly determines the state of the other — even across vast distances. Einstein hated this idea (he called it "spooky action at a distance") but experiments have proven it's real.
In a quantum computer, entanglement is a resource — it means qubits can be correlated in ways that amplify correct answers and cancel out wrong ones when you run a calculation. It's one of the key ingredients that makes quantum computers more powerful than classical ones for specific problems.
中文摘要
量子纠缠可以用一双魔法手套来理解：你把一只装进箱子寄到东京，另一只留在新加坡。打开箱子看到左手套的瞬间，你就知道东京那只是右手套——这并不神奇。但量子纠缠更离奇：在你打开箱子之前，两只手套都还没有决定自己是左还是右！测量其中一个，另一个瞬间"知道"自己的状态，无论相隔多远。爱因斯坦称之为"鬼魅般的远程效应"，但实验已证明它确实存在。在量子计算中，纠缠是一种资源，帮助量子比特相互协调，从而放大正确答案、消除错误答案。
Concept 04 · 概念四
Interference — boosting good answers, killing bad ones
量子干涉——放大正确答案，消除错误答案
The analogy · 类比
Ocean waves 🌊
When two ocean waves meet, they can add together (constructive interference — a bigger wave) or cancel each other out (destructive interference — flat water).

Quantum algorithms use this same trick: they're designed so that paths leading to the correct answer add up, while paths leading to wrong answers cancel each other out. What's left is your answer.
This is why quantum computers aren't just "faster classical computers." They use a fundamentally different trick: interference allows quantum algorithms to be designed so that wrong answers destroy themselves, and the correct answer survives. It's like nature doing the filtering for you.
Constructive vs Destructive Interference — Wave Analogy · 建设性与破坏性干涉
Correct computation paths are reinforced (add together). Wrong paths cancel. The surviving signal is your answer.
中文摘要
量子干涉就像海浪：两列波相遇，可以叠加变大（建设性干涉），也可以相互抵消变平（破坏性干涉）。量子算法正是利用这一原理精心设计——让通向正确答案的路径相互叠加放大，让通向错误答案的路径相互抵消消失。这就是量子计算机与普通计算机的根本区别：它不是更快地逐一枚举答案，而是让错误答案自动消失，正确答案自然浮现。

1
NISQ Era (Now — ~2030) — "Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum." Machines have 50–1,000+ qubits but make errors. Like a calculator that sometimes gives wrong answers. Useful for specific optimisation problems today. IonQ, Rigetti, D-Wave live here.

量子优势阶段：量子机器在商业重要问题上稳定超越经典计算机，纠错能力大幅提升。

Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing (~2033–2040+) — Errors are corrected in real-time. Millions of logical qubits. This is when quantum computers truly change the world — breaking encryption, simulating biology at atomic scale, solving optimisation problems humans cannot even frame today.
容错量子计算阶段：实时纠错，数百万逻辑量子比特，真正改变世界的阶段。
