Position Detail
Memory / HBM — The #1 Choke Point
All three major vendors (SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron) sold out through 2026. HBM now 41% of DRAM revenue, consuming 4× the wafer area per GB and crowding out consumer DRAM. Memory prices up 6× in a year. SemiAnalysis: this is the tightest constraint in the entire stack right now.
Micron (MU) ★ SK Hynix (KRX) Samsung (KRX)
Logic & Foundry — Underpriced Scarcity
TSMC N3 utilisation exceeds 100% in H2 2026. Nvidia overtook Apple as TSMC's largest customer at ~19% of revenue. SemiAnalysis calls TSMC's refusal to raise prices "a strategic error" — meaning latent pricing power exists. Constrained through 2027 with no relief in sight.
TSMC (TSM) ★ NVIDIA (NVDA)
Fab Equipment — TSMC's Bottleneck
TSMC can't add capacity without EUV. Long lead times create durable order books. If TSMC is the bottleneck, ASML is TSMC's bottleneck — arguably more structural pricing power. Lam Research and AMAT capture the etch/deposition steps at every advanced node.
ASML (ASML) ★ Lam Research (LRCX) AMAT KLA (KLAC)
Power — Multi-Year Structural Build
Data centers at 3–4% of US power. Patel's thesis: this needs to 3–5× as AI scales. Power infrastructure takes years to permit and build — the constraint compounds. On-site generation (fuel cells, nuclear offtakes) is the near-term workaround for data centers that can't wait for grid upgrades. Electrician wages have doubled in boom areas.
Vistra (VST) Constellation (CEG) Bloom Energy (BE) GE Vernova (GEV)
Hyperscalers — Value Shifting Here (New)
SemiAnalysis's most recent update marks a regime shift: token demand is price-inelastic, unit economics are improving, and AI labs + hyperscalers are now capturing a disproportionate share of value. Willingness to pay for frontier models is "nearly unbounded." This is the newer leg of the thesis — the infrastructure bottleneck layer is being joined by a model-layer value accrual story.
Microsoft (MSFT) Alphabet (GOOGL) Meta (META) Amazon (AMZN) CoreWeave (CRWV)
Traditional SaaS — Structural Risk
Patel's bear case: AI's high cost of goods sold breaks SaaS economics. When the model is the product, middleware and workflow-wrapper SaaS get squeezed out. Value flows to whoever owns tokens and compute — not to companies charging for "digital transformation" on top. Avoid undifferentiated enterprise SaaS without a clear AI moat.
Salesforce (CRM) ⚠ ServiceNow (NOW) ⚠ Generic SaaS ⚠
⚠ The Patel Bear Case — What to Avoid
Traditional SaaS economics break under AI's high COGS structure. If AI is embedded everywhere, the middleware layer that used to charge for workflow automation gets hollowed out. Value bypasses them and accrues to whoever owns the model and the compute stack. Avoid companies whose moat is integration, not infrastructure or intelligence.
