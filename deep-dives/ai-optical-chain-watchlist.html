Valuation Framework · Honest Assessment

  • Reasonable: Fwd P/E <30× or PEG <0.5 — traditional DCF still applicable
  • Elevated: Fwd P/E 30–60× — requires continued earnings delivery; re-rating risk
  • Extreme: Fwd P/E >60× or TTM >100× — "dream pricing"; sector narrative dominates
  • As of May 2026: AAOI YTD +420%, Lumentum +184%, Innolight +38× since 2022 low — traditional DCF/PE/PB does not apply to most names
  • Relative value still exists: Corning (LTA-backed cash), Fabrinet (EMS discipline), Coherent (valuation discount from segment complexity)

Key Performance Indicators to Track

  • Demand side: Quarterly Capex guidance from top-5 CSPs (MSFT / GOOGL / AMZN / META / ORCL)
  • Technology milestones: NVIDIA GTC / OFC CPO roadmap; 1.6T and 3.2T volume production dates
  • Supply-demand: Optical chip gap (Nomura: ~5–15% undersupply persisting into 2026)
  • Customer concentration: Innolight top-5 = 75.98%; monitor single-customer risk
  • Technology resolution: CPO vs LPO vs NPO vs pluggable final share in scale-up clusters

Key Risks & Stress Scenarios

  • Capex inflection risk: Any hyperscaler guidance cut for 2027 → instant 30–50% multiple compression across the chain
  • CPO substitution: If scale-up commits to co-packaged optics, pluggable module vendors get displaced to scale-out only
  • Geopolitics: China-listed leaders derive 80–90% of revenue overseas; tariff or export control disruption material
  • Technology mis-execution: LPO, CPO, hollow-core fibre commercialisation timelines all carry risk
  • Vertical integration: Hyperscalers developing in-house optics (Amazon, Google) — long-term OEM displacement threat

Technology Roadmap · Key Debates

  • CPO vs Pluggable: Co-packaged optics offer power/density gains but demand silicon photonics maturity; pluggable remains dominant until 2027+
  • LPO (Linear-drive Pluggable): Eliminates DSP; lower cost but tighter link budget — key debate for 800G/1.6T scale-up
  • 1.6T / 3.2T timing: Volume production expected H2 2025–2026; watch qualification announcements
  • Hollow-core fibre: YOFC/others developing air-guided fibre with lower latency; 5–10 year commercialisation horizon
  • Thin-Film LiNbO₃ (TFLN): High-bandwidth modulator candidate for next-gen; still pre-production scale
