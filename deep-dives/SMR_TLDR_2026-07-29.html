Why Hold

1

Only NRC-certified SMR design, but the regulatory moat hasn't converted into revenue

NuScale is the sole small modular reactor developer with a completed U.S. NRC design certification — first at 50 MWe (2023), again at an uprated 77 MWe (2025). Trailing-twelve-month revenue is just $18.7 million against a $2.6 billion market cap, and Q1 2026 revenue collapsed to $565,000 (from $13.4 million a year earlier) as RoPower Romania engineering work rolled off.

2

Binding PPA conversion by year-end 2026 is the single most important catalyst — in either direction

The ENTRA1-TVA (up to 6 GW) and Standard Power (1,848 MWe) frameworks remain explicitly non-binding today. Our Base Case assumes a one-year slip (TVA converts 2027E); Bull (25% probability) has both converting on schedule in H2 2026; Bear (25%) has neither converting by 2030E — a real possibility given NuScale's only prior flagship relationship (UAMPS/Carbon Free Power Project) was cancelled in Nov 2023.

3

Rigorous DCF and trading comps land well below the tape — even after an explicit optionality premium

Scenario-weighted DCF: $2.38/share. Forward trading comps (peer median 5.42x EV/Revenue): $3.42–$3.84/share. We only reach our $7.00 target after layering on a separately disclosed $4.07/share discretionary premium for sector-wide regulatory optionality — the same logic that gives Oklo a ~$4.2bn EV on zero revenue.

4

A $1.25bn cash cushion buys time — at the cost of ~5x dilution and an active fraud suit

Diluted shares have grown from 69 million (2022) to 346 million (2026); Class A authorization doubled to 662 million in Dec 2025. A Q3 2025 disclosure that a $507.4 million ENTRA1 milestone payment drove G&A up >3,000% YoY triggered a pending securities-fraud class action. Even in our Base Case, cash falls to $357 million by 2030E.

DCF and trading comps agree with each other (both land $2–4/share) but both sit far below the tape. We weight DCF 50% / Comps 40% / Precedent Transaction 10% to a mechanical $2.93/share fundamental value, then add a separately disclosed $4.07/share sector-optionality overlay to reach the $7.00 target — a deliberately transparent split between rigorous analysis and analyst judgment.

Reverse sensitivity check

Even an aggressive 65% five-year revenue CAGR combined with a 6.5x exit multiple — above every individual peer's actual trading multiple — produces only $3.03/share on a pure DCF basis. Closing the gap to the current $7.59 price requires the market to keep paying for regulatory optionality the way it does for Oklo, not a change in the fundamental math.

Risk / Catalyst Map

Biggest risks to the rating

  • ENTRA1-TVA/Standard Power fail to convert by year-end 2026. Echoes the 2023 cancellation of the UAMPS/Carbon Free Power Project, NuScale's only prior flagship relationship.
  • Continued shareholder dilution. Dec-2025 authorization doubled Class A shares to 662 million; five-year share count growth already ~5x.
  • Adverse outcome in the pending securities-fraud litigation tied to the ENTRA1/TVA milestone-payment disclosure.
  • Cash burn accelerates beyond model assumptions, forcing an earlier capital raise at a depressed share price.
  • Sector-wide re-rating either direction as AI/data-center power narratives evolve — the whole peer cohort trades on sentiment, not fundamentals.

Watch next

  • Binding PPA execution — ENTRA1/TVA or Standard Power. Management's own year-end-2026 target; the single most important event for the stock.
  • RoPower Romania de-risking. Continued progress toward Doicești construction following the Final Investment Decision already reached.
  • Additional hyperscale/data-center offtake announcements extending the Standard Power precedent to new counterparties.
  • NRC reform / 400 GW-by-2050 policy developments under the current administration's nuclear agenda.
  • Resolution of the pending ENTRA1-related securities litigation on terms that don't materially impair the balance sheet.
