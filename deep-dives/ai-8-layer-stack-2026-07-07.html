1. Framework: Why Eight Layers

The AI trade began as a one-stock story (NVIDIA) and has broadened into a full industrial value chain, segmented into eight layers from physical inputs to end demand:

#LayerWhat it sellsEconomic character
1Semiconductors / AI acceleratorsCompute engines (GPUs, custom ASICs)Oligopoly, extreme margins
2Semi equipment, foundry & memoryThe means of production (fabs, litho, HBM)Monopoly/oligopoly toll roads
3Data center hardware & networkingRacks, switches, optics, coolingCompetitive, mixed margins
4Power & energy infrastructureElectrons and grid capacityScarcity repricing, long duration
5Cloud & AI compute platformsRentable compute (hyperscalers, neoclouds)Scale game, capex-heavy
6Foundation modelsIntelligence itself (mostly private)Land-grab, deeply cash-burning
7Data, tooling & middlewarePlumbing between models and enterprisesFragmented, being re-sorted
8Applications & AI-native softwareWorkflows, agents, copilotsDisruption zone — winners and casualties

Core claim: value accrues fastest where physical scarcity binds (Layers 1, 2, 4) and where distribution plus integration compound (Layer 5); economics are most contested in models and applications (6, 8). Aggregate context: hyperscaler + frontier-lab AI capex tracking toward $550–650B in 2026 EST, up from ~$400B in 2025. Whether that spend earns its cost of capital is the single biggest debate in equities today.

中文摘要 · 框架：为什么是八层 AI 行情已从英伟达一只股票扩展为完整的产业价值链，本报告将其分为八层：芯片、设备/代工/存储、数据中心硬件、电力、云平台、基础模型、数据工具层、应用层。核心观点：价值最快积聚在物理稀缺环节（第1、2、4层）和具备分发+整合优势的云平台（第5层）；模型层和应用层（第6、8层）竞争最激烈、护城河最不确定。2026年AI基础设施资本开支预计达5500–6500亿美元【估算】，其回报率是当前股市最大的争论。

Layer 1 — Semiconductors / AI Accelerators

Why it matters: the compute engines that train and run models — the largest single profit pool in the stack today; the accelerator vendor captures the first and fattest cut of every AI capex dollar.

  • NVIDIA (NVDA) — ~85–90% of merchant AI accelerator revenue EST. Blackwell ramped through 2025; Rubin platform (HBM4) slated for 2026 volume KB Mar-2025. Data center run-rate >$200B annualized exiting 2025 EST; first $5T company (Oct 2025). Moat = CUDA + NVLink rack-scale systems + one-year cadence.
  • AMD — Credible #2; MI400/Helios targeted 2026. OpenAI deal (Oct 2025): 6 GW of GPUs + warrants for ~160M AMD shares KB — transformational validation, but emblematic of vendor-customer entanglement. AI GPU revenue still <10% of NVIDIA's.
  • Broadcom (AVGO) — Custom-ASIC king: Google TPU, Meta MTIA, ~$10B+ OpenAI XPU deal (Sep 2025), 10 GW OpenAI custom silicon (Oct 2025) KB. FY2026 AI semis guided ~$60B+ EST; plus AI networking silicon (Tomahawk 6).
  • Marvell — #2 custom silicon (Trainium, Maia programs) + electro-optics; share-shift questions vs. AVGO.
  • In-house hyperscaler silicon — Google TPU (Gemini trained fully on TPU), Amazon Trainium 2/3 (Anthropic Project Rainier ~1M chips), Microsoft Maia, Meta MTIA — caps NVIDIA's share of internal workloads.
  • China domestic — Huawei Ascend, Cambricon: export-control-capped but improving; "good enough" for domestic inference.

Dynamics: supply constraint migrated to CoWoS packaging + HBM, and increasingly power/shells; custom ASIC unit share ~15–20% (2025) → 25–30% by 2027 EST — grows the pie near term, caps NVIDIA's long-run share. NVIDIA gross margins ~73–75%. NVIDIA captured an estimated ~40–45% of every AI data-center capex dollar in 2025.

Beneficiaries: NVDA (the toll road, rack-scale lock-in) · AVGO (the hedge — wins whether the future is merchant or custom)
中文摘要 · 第1层：半导体 / AI 加速器 这是当前整个价值链最大的利润池。英伟达占商用AI加速器收入约85–90%，凭借 CUDA 生态、NVLink 机架级系统和一年一代的节奏构筑护城河，2025年约每1美元AI数据中心资本开支中拿走40–45美分。博通是定制芯片(ASIC)之王（谷歌TPU、Meta、OpenAI 10GW订单），无论未来走商用GPU还是定制芯片路线都受益。AMD凭借OpenAI 6GW大单成为可信的第二名。风险：定制芯片份额上升（2027年或达25–30%）将长期压制英伟达份额。首选：NVDA、AVGO。

Layer 2 — Semi Equipment, Foundry & Memory

Why it matters: the means of production — every accelerator, merchant or custom, is made by the same handful of companies. The purest "sell shovels to everyone" layer.

  • TSMC (TSM) — Effective monopoly on leading-edge AI logic: ~90%+ of sub-5nm accelerator production EST. N2 ramps through 2026; CoWoS packaging capacity roughly doubling annually and still the gating factor for GPU output. HPC/AI passed 50% of revenue in 2025; ~$165B announced US expansion partially de-risks geography. Structurally under-multipled on the Taiwan-strait discount.
  • ASML — EUV monopoly; High-NA adoption decisions define 2026–28. Orders reaccelerated on AI logic + memory capex upturn KB Q3-2025.
  • AMAT / LRCX / KLAC / TEL — Broad WFE; Lam disproportionately levered to the NAND/DRAM upgrade cycle and HBM etch/deposition.
  • HBM triopoly — SK Hynix ~55–60% HBM share, first to HBM4 qualification for Rubin; Micron (MU) strong #2/#3, HBM sold out through 2026, cleanest US-listed play; Samsung playing HBM4 catch-up.
  • Memory supercycle — late 2025: DRAM/NAND contract prices spiked 30–60%+ q/q in some segments as HBM crowded out standard DRAM capacity KB Nov–Dec 2025. Memory entered 2026 in genuine shortage; HBM consumes ~3x the wafer capacity of standard DRAM per bit.

Risk: classic cyclicality — this layer gets hit first and hardest in any capex digestion phase.

Beneficiaries: TSM (monopoly toll road at a discount multiple) · SK Hynix / MU (HBM4 pricing cycle)
中文摘要 · 第2层：设备、代工与存储 这是"卖铲子给所有人"的一层。台积电垄断先进制程AI芯片代工（5nm以下约90%+），CoWoS先进封装仍是全行业GPU产量的瓶颈；ASML垄断EUV光刻。HBM存储呈三寡头格局：SK海力士领先（约55–60%份额），美光是最纯正的美股标的，HBM已售罄至2026年。2025年底常规内存价格暴涨30–60%，2026年进入真正的存储短缺周期。主要风险是周期性——资本开支一旦消化，此层最先、最重地受创。首选：TSM、美光/SK海力士。注意：实时数据显示美光市值已达约1.1万亿美元，此轮涨价逻辑已大幅兑现（见第9a节）。

Layer 3 — Data Center Hardware & Networking

Why it matters: turns chips into deployable systems. As clusters scale to hundreds of thousands of accelerators, the network and the thermal system become the product. Rack power moved from ~10kW (cloud era) to 130kW+ (GB200/NVL72), heading to megawatt-class racks (Rubin Ultra, ~2027) — liquid cooling is now mandatory.

  • Servers: Dell (AI servers >$20B annualized, CoreWeave/xAI wins, but low-teens gross margin pass-through); Super Micro (share and margin compression after 2024–25 accounting turmoil); Foxconn/Quanta/Wiwynn (the real volume rack builders); Celestica (hyperscaler ODM winner).
  • Networking: Arista (ANET) — Ethernet won vs. InfiniBand for scale-out AI fabrics; Meta/Microsoft/Oracle anchors; $10B+ revenue framework. NVIDIA (Spectrum-X, NVLink) is both biggest competitor and biggest attach.
  • Optics: Coherent, Lumentum (800G→1.6T cycle), Credo (AECs), Astera Labs (PCIe/CXL, Scorpio). Watch co-packaged optics shifting value from pluggables to the switch late-decade.
  • Power/cooling in the rack: Vertiv (VRT) — the pure-play on liquid cooling + power distribution; book-to-bill consistently >1.3x through 2025.

Margin bifurcation: commodity box assembly (low-teens GM) vs. differentiated content (ANET ~64% GM, VRT margin expansion, optics in shortage). Own the differentiated content; customer concentration is extreme layer-wide.

Beneficiaries: VRT (chip-agnostic content per rack rises every generation) · ANET (Ethernet share + 1.6T cycle)
中文摘要 · 第3层：数据中心硬件与网络 随着集群规模扩大到几十万颗芯片，网络和散热系统本身成为核心产品：单机架功率从云时代约10kW升至130kW以上，液冷已成必需。价值向差异化环节集中：Arista（AI以太网龙头，毛利约64%）、Vertiv（液冷+配电纯正标的，订单出货比持续大于1.3倍）、光模块（800G→1.6T升级周期）；而组装服务器（戴尔、超微）是低毛利过手生意。该层普遍存在客户高度集中风险。首选：VRT（无论谁的芯片赢，机架含量都在上升）、ANET。

Layer 4 — Power & Energy Infrastructure

Why it matters: the newest binding constraint — chips can be manufactured faster than gigawatts can be built. US data-center load ~4–5% of electricity (2024) with credible paths to ~9–12% by 2030 EST. "Time to power" — not chips — became the #1 cited constraint during 2025.

  • Generators/nuclear: Constellation (largest US nuclear fleet; Microsoft Three Mile Island restart PPA, target 2027–28; Calpine acquisition ~$27B); Vistra (nuclear + ERCOT gas — the most AI-levered power market); Talen (AWS Susquehanna); NRG (GE Vernova JV).
  • Equipment: GE Vernova (GEV) — heavy-duty gas turbines effectively sold out into 2028–29 with slot-reservation payments and rising pricing; grid equipment (transformers, HVDC) in structural shortage. Also Siemens Energy, Eaton, Schneider, Quanta Services.
  • Next-gen: Bloom Energy (bridge fuel cells); SMRs (Oklo, NuScale) are ~2030+ options, not earnings; natural gas is the realistic marginal fuel for AI load 2026–2030.
  • Regulated utilities: Dominion, AEP, Southern, Duke, Entergy (Meta's Louisiana campus) — slower-burn rate-base growth.

Dynamics: turbine/transformer lead times 3–5+ years; hyperscalers signing 10–20 year premium PPAs and going behind-the-meter — a structural repricing of firm, clean capacity. Key risk: the trade is consensus and crowded; power names de-rate violently on any capex scare (Jan-2025 DeepSeek episode hit VST/CEG hardest).

Beneficiaries: GEV (sold-out turbines + grid = visibility through 2028) · CEG or VST (firm capacity repriced via hyperscaler PPAs)
中文摘要 · 第4层：电力与能源基础设施 电力是最新的硬约束——造芯片比建电厂快。"拿到电的时间"取代芯片成为2025年AI扩建的头号瓶颈；美国数据中心用电占比或从4–5%升至2030年的9–12%。GE Vernova燃气轮机已售罄至2028–29年且持续提价；核电（Constellation、Vistra）通过与超大规模云厂商签订10–20年溢价购电协议实现资产重估。风险：该交易已成共识且拥挤，任何资本开支恐慌都会引发剧烈回调（参考2025年1月DeepSeek事件）。首选：GEV、CEG/VST。

Layer 5 — Cloud & AI Compute Platforms

Why it matters: aggregates Layers 1–4 into rentable compute and distributes AI to enterprises. This layer writes the checks that fund everything below it — its returns discipline is the system's thermostat.

  • Microsoft — Azure high-30s% growth through 2025, AI-driven; restructured OpenAI deal (Oct 2025): ~27% of the OpenAI PBC + IP rights into the 2030s.
  • Amazon — AWS reaccelerated to ~20%+; Trainium 2 + Anthropic (Project Rainier) counters "AWS is behind"; Bedrock as neutral marketplace.
  • Alphabet (GOOGL) — The most vertically integrated stack in the world: TPU → GCP → Gemini → Search/Workspace distribution. Gemini 3 (Nov 2025) flipped the narrative to "Google may be winning"; Cloud growth 30%+.
  • Oracle (ORCL) — High-beta expression: RPO ~$455B after the ~$300B/5-yr OpenAI contract; market then interrogated single-counterparty risk, thin OCI margins, leverage/CDS spreads. Effectively a leveraged bet on OpenAI's ability to pay.
  • Meta — Top-3 capex spender (2026 >$100B guided) with no external compute revenue — the most scrutinized ROI story; novel off-balance-sheet financing (Hyperion JV).
  • Neoclouds — CoreWeave (~$30B+ backlog incl. OpenAI $22B+, Meta ~$14B; heavy GPU-collateralized debt), Nebius (Microsoft up to ~$19.4B), Lambda/Crusoe/Together. Essentially GPU landlords financed with debt against depreciating collateral — first casualties if utilization or residuals disappoint. Stargate (~$500B ambition) is the emblem of scale and financing complexity.

Debates: big-4 capex ~$400B (2025) → ~$550–650B (2026) EST, all citing power/shell constraints, not demand. Depreciation debate: 5–6-year server lives vs. critics' 2–3-year competitive GPU lives — directly drives whether cloud AI margins are real. Inference passing 50% of workloads favors owned silicon and improves revenue quality.

Beneficiaries: GOOGL (full-stack integration finally priced as an advantage) · MSFT (distribution + enterprise default). High-beta: CRWV.
中文摘要 · 第5层：云与AI算力平台 云平台层是整个体系的"出资方"，其投资纪律是系统的恒温器。2026年四大厂资本开支预计5500–6500亿美元，均表示瓶颈在电力和机房而非需求。谷歌是全球唯一全栈自有者（TPU→云→Gemini→分发），Gemini 3之后叙事已反转；微软持有OpenAI约27%股权+IP授权；甲骨文4550亿美元RPO实质是对OpenAI付款能力的杠杆押注；CoreWeave等"新云"是用债务购买快速贬值GPU的房东，是任何风险事件的第一批伤亡者。关键争论：GPU折旧年限（5–6年 vs 2–3年）直接决定云厂商盈利的真实性。首选：GOOGL、MSFT。

Layer 6 — Foundation Models

Why it matters: where capability is created and where, in theory, the largest long-run value should sit. Almost entirely private — public access is indirect (MSFT→OpenAI, GOOGL→DeepMind, AMZN/GOOGL→Anthropic).

  • OpenAI — ~$500B valuation (Oct 2025 secondary); revenue run-rate ~$13–20B exiting 2025 EST; >$1T cumulative compute commitments across Oracle/Microsoft/AMD/Broadcom/CoreWeave/Stargate — the central node of the circular-financing debate. PBC restructuring cleared the IPO path; ChatGPT ~800M weekly users.
  • Anthropic — $183B post-money (Sep 2025), reported talks at $300–350B; run-rate passed ~$7B in 2025 with the steepest enterprise slope; strongest in coding (Claude Code) and enterprise API; compute via AWS Trainium + Google TPU (~1M TPUs deal).
  • Google DeepMind — Gemini 3 judged frontier-leading or co-leading; the only lab with owned silicon-to-distribution integration.
  • Meta — $14.3B Scale AI deal + researcher poaching; pivoted from pure open-weights toward closed frontier work.
  • xAI — Colossus (1M+ GPU ambitions), merged with X, funding talks at $200B+.
  • Open-weights insurgents — DeepSeek (whose R1 caused the ~$600B one-day NVDA drawdown, Jan 2025), Alibaba Qwen, Mistral (~$14B) — anchor the commoditization bear case: frontier capability, 6–12 months later, near-zero price.

Dynamics: marginal compute dollar shifting to inference-time reasoning + RL post-training; all frontier labs deeply cash-flow negative (OpenAI burn reportedly tens of billions through 2028–29); token prices deflate 10x+ every 12–18 months. Moats shifting from raw model quality to distribution, enterprise trust, and agentic surfaces. Read-through: own the labs via their public equity holders rather than paying private-round marks.

Beneficiaries (public proxies): GOOGL (owns its lab outright) · MSFT (~27% of OpenAI PBC + IP rights)
中文摘要 · 第6层：基础模型 智能创造层，理论上长期价值最大，但几乎全部为私有公司：OpenAI估值约5000亿美元（对外算力承诺超1万亿美元，是"循环融资"争论的核心节点）；Anthropic估值1830亿并传闻谈至3000–3500亿，企业收入增速最陡；谷歌DeepMind是唯一从芯片到分发全自有的实验室。所有前沿实验室现金流均深度为负，代币价格每12–18个月通缩10倍以上，DeepSeek、Qwen等开源模型支撑"模型商品化"的空头论点。结论：通过公开股东（GOOGL、MSFT）间接持有模型层，勿以私募估值接盘。

Layer 7 — Data, Tooling & Middleware

Why it matters: the connective tissue — getting enterprise data to models, orchestrating agents, observing what they do. Being re-sorted: foundation models absorb some functions even as agents create new needs.

  • Palantir (PLTR) — The breakout: AIP made it the default "make AI work inside an enterprise/government" vendor; US commercial growth >60–100% y/y. The problem is price: entered 2026 at roughly 60–75x forward sales EST — separate thesis from valuation ruthlessly.
  • Snowflake — successful AI-era repositioning (Cortex); growth ~25–30%; competes with private Databricks (>$100B valuation, ~$4B run-rate) which owns more AI-native workload.
  • MDB / DDOG / CFLT / Elastic — genuine but second-derivative exposure; DDOG added LLM observability; MDB's vector search squeezed standalone vector DBs.
  • Vector/RAG specialists — cautionary tale: vector search became a database feature within 18 months; standalone value eroded.
  • Agent tooling — LangChain/LlamaIndex strategically important, economically small; Anthropic's MCP became the de facto agent-integration standard in 2025 — advantaging model vendors, not middleware startups.
  • Data labeling / RL environments — Scale AI (post-Meta), Surge (>$1B, profitable), Mercor — quietly enormous private revenue.

Bull: agents multiply demand for context, memory, observability, governance. Bear: the model layer eats the middleware. Both partly true — buy data gravity, avoid glue.

Beneficiaries: PLTR (fundamental winner — flag valuation loudly) · SNOW / private Databricks (the enterprise data-to-AI gateway)
中文摘要 · 第7层：数据、工具与中间件 连接企业数据与模型的"结缔组织"，正在被重新洗牌：模型层不断吞噬中间件功能（长上下文取代RAG管道、模型厂商自带编排），但智能体又创造新需求（记忆、可观测、治理）。Palantir是基本面赢家但估值极端（约60–75倍远期市销率）；Snowflake/Databricks占据企业数据入口；独立向量数据库18个月内沦为大厂"功能"，是警示案例。选股原则：买有数据引力的平台，避开卖"胶水"的点状工具。首选：PLTR（须大声提示估值风险）、SNOW。

Layer 8 — Applications & AI-Native Software

Why it matters: where AI meets end demand — the market is repricing both the biggest opportunity (AI-native franchises) and the biggest risk (agents disrupting seat-based SaaS). 2025's "SaaS-mageddon" de-rating was this layer's defining event.

  • Coding agents — the first killer app: Cursor/Anysphere (~$29B, Nov 2025; ~$1B run-rate), GitHub Copilot, Claude Code (>$1B run-rate within months), Replit, Cognition. Real revenue and productivity, brutal competition, questionable gross margins (thin/negative spread reselling frontier tokens).
  • Incumbent copilots: Microsoft (widest distribution in software history); ServiceNow (NOW) — best-in-class AI SKU attach, ~$1B+ AI ACV EST; Salesforce — Agentforce monetization underwhelmed, de-rated to ~20x fwd EPS: cheap compounder or structurally disrupted?; Adobe — Firefly real but market prices generative video as existential; ~15–17x.
  • AI-native consumer: ChatGPT (the franchise), Perplexity (~$18–20B), Midjourney, ElevenLabs; Duolingo/AppLovin as public AI-leverage proof points.
  • Vertical AI: Harvey (legal), Abridge/OpenEvidence (health), Sierra (~$10B) — the fastest 0→$100M ARR cohort in software history.

Dynamics: seats → outcomes/consumption pricing transition; AI-native gross margins 40–60% vs. classic SaaS 80%+ (model-price deflation helps yearly); the labor-budget TAM argument ($30T payroll vs. $1T software) holds only if reliability crosses the delegation threshold. Value bifurcating violently — accruing to distribution-rich incumbents and escape-velocity AI-natives; eroding for undifferentiated seat-based SaaS. Stock selection matters most here; index exposure works worst.

Beneficiaries: NOW (cleanest incumbent AI monetizer) · MSFT (distribution). Contrarian watch: CRM / ADBE as priced-for-disruption options.
中文摘要 · 第8层：应用与AI原生软件 AI与终端需求的交汇处，也是2025年"SaaS大杀跌"的震中。编程智能体是第一个杀手级应用（Cursor估值290亿、Claude Code数月内收入破10亿美元），但普遍毛利存疑。现有软件商两极分化：ServiceNow是将AI变现最干净的在位者；Salesforce、Adobe被市场按"结构性被颠覆"定价（仅15–22倍市盈率），是逆向期权。商业模式正从按席位收费转向按结果/用量收费；AI原生应用毛利仅40–60%。该层选股最重要、指数化最失效。首选：NOW、MSFT。实时数据：NOW已较52周高点回撤约49%，回调远超报告基准假设（见第9a节）。

10. Cross-Stack Synthesis

Where the profit pool sits today

  1. Layer 1 (NVIDIA + Broadcom) — the overwhelming majority of current economic profit; NVIDIA alone likely earns more AI operating profit than Layers 3–8 combined.
  2. Layer 2 (TSMC, HBM, equipment) — second-largest and most durable (everyone's supplier).
  3. Layer 5 (hyperscalers) — large gross profits netted against enormous capex; true ROIC unresolved.
  4. Layer 4 (power) — smaller pool but the fastest repricing of existing assets.
  5. Layers 3, 7 — real but selective (content-per-rack winners; data-gravity platforms).
  6. Layers 6, 8 — massive value creation, negative aggregate cash flow. The stack's paradox mirrors 1999 (infrastructure profitable, applications not) — a pattern that then inverted over a decade. Positioning: own scarcity layers for the current regime; own distribution/integration (MSFT, GOOGL) for the inversion.

The key debates

  • Capex sustainability & circularity — ~$600B of 2026 spend rests substantially on unprofitable labs' revenue plans; NVIDIA→OpenAI→Oracle→NVIDIA circularity; AMD pays OpenAI in warrants. Our read: more demand-backed than 2000-era telecom (contracted RPO, ~100% utilization), but the financing chain — not demand — is where an accident starts. Watch OCI margins, CRWV refis, lab fundraising terms.
  • Inference vs. training — inference >50% favors custom silicon, memory, power, and makes revenue recurring; marginally negative for NVIDIA share.
  • Custom ASIC vs. merchant GPU — ASICs toward 25–30% of units by 2027, concentrated at the 5 biggest buyers; both NVDA and AVGO can win; the loser is anyone in between without scale.
  • Model commoditization — open-weights at frontier-minus-6-months and near-zero price; if intelligence commoditizes, value flows to distribution and physical scarcity — where we already recommend positioning.
  • Depreciation & earnings quality (the sleeper) — 3-year competitive GPU life would overstate hyperscaler EPS mid-to-high single digits and crush neocloud equity; A100 longevity favors the longer-life view, but Rubin-era efficiency could compress residuals abruptly.

Biggest risks

  • Capex air-pocket — one hyperscaler blinking on 2027 guidance de-rates Layers 1–4 by 20–30% (Jan-2025 DeepSeek was the fire drill).
  • Financing-chain accident — OpenAI's ~$1T+ commitments vs. revenue; Oracle/CoreWeave leverage; GPU-collateralized private credit.
  • Power/permitting slippage delaying revenue recognition stack-wide.
  • Geopolitics — Taiwan concentration is un-hedgeable within the theme; export-control escalation either direction.
  • Model-efficiency shock — another 10x algorithmic gain (long-term Jevons, short-term drawdown).
  • Concentration — the theme is ~40%+ of S&P 500 cap: theme risk is now market risk.
