Headline 核心结论

Bloom Energy delivered a large beat-and-raise quarter that directly challenges the core pillars of our SELL thesis. Q2 FY2026 revenue reached $1.065B, +166% YoY and +42% QoQ — an acceleration versus the +130% YoY growth rate we cited as already-elevated in our initiation report, not the deceleration our bear case assumed. Non-GAAP EPS of $0.78 beat consensus of $0.40 by $0.38 (~95%). Management raised full-year guidance across revenue, EPS, and operating income. This is the single most consequential data point since our 21 Jul 2026 initiation and requires us to flag our $58.00 price target and FY2026 model estimates as stale pending a full refresh.

Oracle Deal & Backlog 甲骨文合作与订单储备

  • Management reported delivering power to an Oracle data center within 55 days of first engagement, and cited validation from additional major hyperscalers plus more than a dozen neoclouds, AI labs, and colocation operators.
  • Press coverage citing company disclosures references backlog figures in the $20B range with continued expansion tied to the Oracle relationship, though exact backlog composition and the split between the original and expanded Oracle commitments is not fully disaggregated in earnings-release-level disclosure we reviewed — flagged as a data point needing 10-Q-level verification, not yet independently confirmed to the same standard as our initiation report.
中文摘要 管理层表示已在与甲骨文首次接触后的55天内为其数据中心供电，并获得其他大型云服务商及十余家新兴云厂商、AI实验室和数据中心托管商的验证。媒体报道援引公司披露称订单储备规模在200亿美元左右，但具体构成及原始与扩展后甲骨文承诺的拆分未在财报层面充分披露，仍需通过10-Q文件做进一步核实。

Hunterbrook Litigation — Unresolved Overhang Hunterbrook诉讼：尚未解决的风险

  • On 8 Jul 2026, Hunterbrook Media (a self-disclosed short position holder) published allegations that Bloom sourced scandium oxide from China via intermediary countries while publicly denying China dependence, with follow-on allegations of hardware degradation and Oracle project delays. The stock fell as much as 12% intraday, closing down 5.7%.
  • A securities class action was filed 30 Jul 2026 in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, covering purchasers from 27 Feb 2025 through 8 Jul 2026.
  • Bloom has categorically denied the claims, stating its scandium oxide supply is sufficient for current backlog and demand and is not dependent on China.
  • This risk is unresolved and sits outside our original SELL thesis, which was built on valuation and related-party/leverage concerns, not disclosure-fraud risk. It is a genuine addition to the risk register, not a confirmation of our existing bear case.
中文摘要 2026年7月8日，做空机构Hunterbrook Media发布报告，指控Bloom通过中间国采购中国产钪同时公开否认对中国的依赖，并追加指控硬件性能衰退及甲骨文项目延迟。股价盘中一度下跌12%，收盘跌5.7%。2026年7月30日，一项证券集体诉讼在加州北区联邦地方法院提起，涵盖2025年2月27日至2026年7月8日期间的买入者。Bloom已明确否认相关指控。这一风险尚未解决，且不属于我们最初SELL评级逻辑（基于估值和关联方/杠杆担忧）所涵盖的范畴，是风险清单中真正的新增项，而非对既有看空逻辑的验证。

Thesis Impact & Recommended Next Step 对论文的影响与下一步建议

This print is a significant negative data point for our SELL thesis on fundamentals: growth accelerated rather than decelerated, and guidance was raised rather than held or cut — the opposite of what a "backlog-conversion disappoints" bear case requires. At the same time, none of the valuation, leverage (debt/equity remains elevated), or related-party-concentration concerns from our initiation report have been resolved, and a new, material, unresolved risk (the Hunterbrook litigation) has emerged that argues for caution independent of the operating beat.

We are not issuing a revised rating or price target in this note. Doing so without rebuilding the DCF and comps model against the new guidance — and without more clarity on the Hunterbrook litigation outcome — would not meet the standard we held ourselves to in the initiation report. The next deliverable should be a full model refresh incorporating the raised guidance, followed by a re-underwritten valuation and an explicit rating decision.

中文摘要 从基本面角度看，本季度业绩对我们的SELL评级逻辑构成显著负面冲击：增长不降反升，指引不降反升——这与"订单转化不及预期"的看空逻辑假设正好相反。与此同时，我们首次覆盖报告中提出的估值、杠杆（负债权益比仍处高位）及关联方集中度担忧均未得到解决，且新出现了尚未解决的重大风险（Hunterbrook诉讼），这本身也构成独立于本季度经营超预期之外的谨慎理由。本次点评不给出修订后的评级或目标价——在未用新指引重建DCF及可比公司模型、且Hunterbrook诉讼结果尚不明朗的情况下给出评级调整，将无法达到我们在首次覆盖报告中为自己设定的标准。下一步交付物应为纳入上调后指引的完整模型更新，随后进行重新估值并明确给出评级决定。
