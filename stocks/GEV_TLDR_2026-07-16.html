The Debate

What's real

  • Gas Power backlog: 83→100GW in Q1'26, targeting 110GW by YE26; orders +71% organic.
  • Electrification + Prolec GE: fastest-growing segment, 16% 5-yr CAGR, transformer scarcity pricing.
  • BWRX-300 SMR: genuine first-mover Western SMR, construction underway at Darlington.

What's already priced in

  • Valuation gap: 3.5–4.5x EV/EBITDA premium to closest peers, unexplained by growth edge alone.
  • Wind still bleeding: -6.6% EBITDA margin, Vineyard Wind 1 dispute >$300mm unpaid.
  • Two methods agree: DCF ($268) and comps ($272) independently land near the same number.

Catalysts & Risks

Upside Catalysts

  1. Backlog conversion outpaces 110GW target
  2. BWRX-300 clears NRC / OPG milestones on schedule
  3. Wind turns EBITDA-positive ahead of 2029E
  4. More hyperscaler JVs beyond Chevron

Downside Risks

  1. AI capex digestion slows gas turbine orders
  2. Siemens Energy / Mitsubishi Power capacity compresses pricing, 2027–28
  3. Further offshore wind losses or disputes
  4. Prolec GE integration missteps ($5.25bn, largest deal to date)

Bottom Line

Both a bottom-up five-year DCF ($268/share base case) and an eight-peer trading-comps cross-check ($272/share median) land in the same neighborhood — roughly 73% below today's $1,068 price. This isn't a call that the AI/electrification thesis is wrong; Gas Power, Prolec GE, and BWRX-300 are genuine structural drivers. It's a call that the price already assumes an unusually complete, prolonged realization of that thesis, with a Wind segment still bleeding and little margin of safety if execution slips anywhere across the three-segment portfolio.
