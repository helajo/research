The Debate

Bull Case

  • Gas Power backlog approaching 110GW by year-end 2026, still converting.
  • Prolec GE consolidation strengthens Electrification amid a genuine transformer shortage.
  • BWRX-300 SMR has real first-mover status among Western nuclear vendors.
  • Fastest revenue growth in the peer set (16% vs. ~9% peer median).
  • Hyperscaler JV structure (2026 Chevron deal) is a repeatable growth vector.

Bear Case

  • 3.5–4.5x EV/EBITDA premium to closest direct peers is extreme even among "AI power" names.
  • Even the most aggressive DCF corner (6.8% WACC, 4.0% terminal growth) tops out at $610 — 43% below today's price.
  • Gas turbine oligopoly (GEV, Siemens Energy, Mitsubishi Power) is simultaneously adding capacity into 2027–2028.
  • Wind segment turnaround still incomplete; offshore contract disputes ongoing.
  • Prolec GE is GEV's largest deal as an independent company, funded with real new leverage.

Catalysts & Risks

Catalysts (Upside Risk)

  1. Backlog conversion outpacing the 110GW target.
  2. BWRX-300 clears NRC / Ontario Power Generation milestones on schedule.
  3. Wind reaches positive EBITDA ahead of 2029E–2030E schedule.
  4. Additional hyperscaler joint ventures beyond the Chevron structure.
  5. Continued sell-side estimate raises narrowing the multiple gap.

Risks (Downside Risk)

  1. AI capex digestion reducing turbine and grid equipment orders.
  2. Oligopoly capacity additions compress pricing power by 2027–2028.
  3. Further offshore wind losses or contract disputes (Vineyard Wind 1-style).
  4. Tariff escalation on steel, aluminum, copper outpacing contract repricing.
  5. Prolec GE integration missteps on newly issued leverage.

Bottom Line

We initiate at SELL / UNDERWEIGHT with a ~$290 12-month price target (~73% downside), a 55%/45% blend of a bottom-up DCF ($268/share base case) and an eight-peer trading-comps cross-check ($317/share median). The thesis isn't that GEV's growth story is fake — it's that the current price already assumes an unusually complete version of it plays out, with little room for execution slippage.
