What Each Company Makes — The Product Stack Compared
产品栈对比 — 广度与深度的差异
Both companies make optical and photonic products for AI data centres. But their strategies are fundamentally different: COHR is a platform — owning the entire value chain from raw material to finished system. LITE is a specialist — going deeper in fewer areas, with world-beating market share at the component level.

The critical product difference is in EML chips: Lumentum holds 50–60% global market share in 200G-per-lane EMLs — the most supply-constrained component in AI optical infrastructure. Even Coherent, its direct competitor, may buy EML chips from Lumentum for certain applications because LITE's production scale and epitaxy quality are ahead. CEO Michael Hurlston stated Lumentum is undershipping customer demand by roughly 30%, with all EML capacity locked under long-term agreements through calendar 2027 — customers who won't sign LTAs risk losing supply priority entirely. This seller's market dynamic for a monopoly-adjacent component is extraordinarily rare and valuable.
Coherent's advantage is breadth and integration — it can supply a data centre operator with everything from the InP wafer through the complete system, capturing margin at every step. This makes COHR the preferred vendor for customers who want a single integrated optical supply chain partner. LITE is preferred by customers who specifically need the highest-quality EML chips and OCS systems — and are willing to pay premium prices for them.

SECTION 5 · 第五节
The Moat Deep Dive — Epitaxy, EMLs, and OCS
护城河深度剖析 — 外延生长、EML与光电路交换
The Epitaxy Moat (LITE's deepest advantage): Both companies grow InP crystals in-house — but Lumentum's epitaxy capability, built through decades of JDSU, Oclaro, and NeoPhotonics heritage, produces InP wafers of exceptional optical quality. Lumentum organises its business into Components ($444M in Q2 FY2026, +68% YoY) and Systems ($222M, +60% YoY). Laser chips are fabricated in indium phosphide wafer fabs in San Jose and Japan. The quality of these chips — particularly the uniformity of the active layer — is what allows Lumentum's 200G EMLs to achieve the signal integrity required at 1.6T speeds. Coherent also grows InP, but Lumentum's history in high-performance laser chips is longer and its quality control in this specific application has proven superior at current production volumes.
EML Market Position — Lumentum's Crown Jewel: Lumentum holds an estimated 50–60% global market share in high-end EML laser chips and is currently the only supplier shipping 200G-per-lane EMLs at volume — the critical component for 1.6T transceivers. NVIDIA pre-allocated laser chip capacity at major suppliers, pushing delivery timelines for competitors past 2027. This is not a sustainable monopoly in perpetuity — Coherent and others will scale their own 200G EML production — but through 2027–2028, Lumentum is effectively the only option for customers who need 1.6T at scale. This seller's market creates pricing power that shows directly in the 47.9% non-GAAP gross margin.
OCS — Lumentum's Next Growth Vector: In OCS, Lumentum is scaling rapidly to meet extraordinary customer demand that has already driven backlog well beyond $400 million. In CPO, the company received an incremental multi-hundred-million-dollar order, deliverable in first half calendar 2027. OCS surpassed $10M per quarter ahead of schedule in Q2 FY2026, and Q3 systems revenue of $275M (+121% YoY) reflects both transceiver scaling and OCS ramp. The CEO explicitly stated the company is "only at the starting line" for OCS and CPO — both are multi-billion-dollar future markets where Lumentum already has confirmed orders and production partnerships.
CPO — Coherent's Counter-Claim: COHR demonstrated a 6.4T socketed SiPho CPO system at OFC 2026 and has NVIDIA's explicit support across five CPO product families. On CPO, the race between COHR and LITE is genuinely too close to call — both have strong positions, both have NVIDIA backing, and neither has yet recognised material CPO revenue. This is the key "unknown" in both investment cases. The company that wins the CPO design win at NVIDIA's next-generation switch ASIC (likely 2027–2028 volume) will have a decisive moat for 3–5 years.

Bull & Bear Cases for Each
各自的多头与空头情景

COHR — Bull & Bear

🐂 COHR Bull — Target $550–650
CPO design win at NVIDIA's next-gen switch ASIC — Coherent's demonstrated 6.4T SiPho CPO system and NVIDIA's $2B investment + 5-product-family access puts it in prime position to win the defining CPO contract of 2027. Vertical integration allows it to capture the entire value chain at higher unit volumes. Industrial recovery adds €300–400M incremental high-margin revenue. Debt continues declining; GAAP profitability emerges in FY2027, unlocking a new class of institutional buyers currently excluded by GAAP losses.
🐻 COHR Bear — Target $180–240
CPO loses to LITE or a third competitor — most damaging single event possible. GAAP losses from $7.6B goodwill persist longer than expected, maintaining the earnings quality overhang. AI capex cycle pauses; transceiver demand normalises while COHR carries heavy debt service. Shares outstanding grew 17% in one year from NVIDIA placement + SBC — dilution prevents per-share value creation. Stock at $382 is 49% above NVIDIA's basis with no earnings floor.

LITE — Bull & Bear

🐂 LITE Bull — Target $1,400–1,800
OCS becomes a $500M+ annual revenue business by FY2028 as Google, Microsoft, and Meta deploy all-optical AI cluster backbones — LITE is the only proven volume OCS supplier. CPO multi-hundred-million order delivers H1 2027 and scales to a $300–500M run-rate. EML demand continues to outstrip supply through 2028, sustaining 47–50%+ gross margins. Non-GAAP EPS reaches $12–15 by FY2028, justifying $1,200–1,500+ at 30x earnings. Nasdaq-100 inclusion (May 18, 2026) drives passive fund buying. Net cash of $3.17B deployed in earnings-accretive buybacks.
🐻 LITE Bear — Target $400–550
EML supply catches up with demand as Coherent, II-VI heritage fabs, and Asian competitors scale 200G EML production by 2027 — pricing power evaporates, gross margin compresses from 48% back toward 38–40%. The stock at $970 is pricing in sustained EML monopoly economics; any sign of supply normalisation triggers severe multiple compression. OCS volumes slower than expected — $400M backlog but Q4 ramp disappoints. CPO delayed to 2029+. The 1,267% move from 52-week lows leaves little margin for error. Stock falls back toward 20–25x a lower EPS trajectory.

SECTION 8 · 第八节
Investment Verdict — Who Should Own What
投资结论 — 谁适合持有哪一只
Own COHR if you…
Scale + Breadth

• Want the widest exposure to 光领域 through one stock — COHR touches every part of the optical stack including SiC, GaAs, DCI, and industrial lasers that LITE doesn't cover

• Are comfortable with GAAP complexity — can distinguish merger-era accounting artefacts from operating performance

• Want to pay less on P/S (0.9x vs 2.5x) and EV/EBITDA for the same AI optical tailwind

• Believe COHR's CPO demonstration at OFC 2026 means it wins the decisive NVIDIA design cycle

• Have a 3+ year horizon for GAAP profitability and debt normalisation to unlock value

• Can tolerate beta of 2.053 — stock will swing violently on any AI capex news
Own LITE if you…
Focus + Quality

• Want the highest-quality, highest-margin business in 光领域 with a genuine component monopoly

• Value balance sheet strength — $3.17B net cash means no dilution risk, no debt service drag, and capital available for buybacks

• Prioritise margin quality — 47.9% non-GAAP gross margin is among the highest in all of semiconductor equipment and photonics

• Want lower volatility (beta 1.533 vs 2.053) for a large-cap-quality position in the AI optical theme

• Believe the EML seller's market — and OCS — sustain pricing power through 2027–2028

• Want analyst consensus targets that actually match the current price (~$1,034–1,081 vs $970 current)
The single most important conclusion: If you can own only one, own LITE for quality — higher margins, net cash, genuine EML monopoly, lower beta, GAAP profitable, and analyst targets matching current prices. Own COHR as the complement — it gives you exposure LITE doesn't cover (SiC, GaAs, DCI coherent, industrial scale) at a cheaper P/S multiple, with the CPO wild card still open. The ideal 光领域 portfolio for your boss holds both: LITE as the 3–4% core position (quality anchor), COHR as the 2–3% satellite (breadth + CPO optionality). Together they cover essentially all of Layer 2's optical supply chain with different but complementary risk profiles.
