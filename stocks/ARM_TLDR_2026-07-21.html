Why Sell

1

A near-monopoly franchise, priced past every peer

~99% of smartphone CPUs run Arm; ~50% of hyperscaler cloud compute does too. Real moat — but the stock trades at 172.9x our FY27E EBITDA, above every name in an 8-company set that includes Nvidia (25.2x) and Broadcom (44.8x), despite below-median growth and margin.

2

The AGI CPU pivot is real — and still unproven

Arm's first physical-silicon product (co-developed with Meta) has a disclosed >$20bn demand pipeline, but only ~$1bn of secured TSMC capacity as of the trigger for a same-day 7% share drop in May 2026. Our Base Case models just $2.9bn of FY31E silicon revenue — 19% of management's own $15bn target.

3

Independent methods converge well below market price

DCF ($12–$33), comps ($45–$72), and a precedent transaction ($85–$114) — three methods, built separately, none reverse-engineered to a target — average to $50.33. Convergence, not a single model, is why conviction is high.

4

A margin structure investors haven't stress-tested yet

Gross margin has never dipped below 95% in Arm's reporting history. Our model shows it compressing to 86.0% by FY31E as silicon's real foundry costs dilute a business that used to be almost pure IP.

Reverse-DCF check

Solving for the perpetual growth rate the current price implies, at our ~19.9% base-case WACC, returns roughly 19.5% — not a sustainable literal input to a Gordon Growth model. Read correctly: the market is pricing a growth window well beyond our FY31E forecast, and/or near-full delivery of management's $15bn chip-revenue aspiration. Neither is our base case.

Risk / Catalyst Map

Biggest risks to the SELL

  • Momentum persists. SoftBank owns ~87%; low float can keep a story stock disconnected from fundamentals for a long time.
  • Capacity resolves fast. A rapid TSMC allocation could re-rate the AGI CPU line toward Bull Case.
  • AI capex stays hot. Data-center royalty is our highest-growth, most capex-sensitive line.
  • Beta normalizes. A lower realized volatility mechanically lifts every DCF output here.
  • $15bn target lands in full. We model 19–53% of it; full delivery breaks our math.

Watch next

  • Jul 29, 2026 — Q1 FY27 earnings. First real read on TSMC capacity progress.
  • Qualcomm appeal outcome. Loss validates licensee pushback risk on royalty terms.
  • Named AGI CPU customers. Pipeline logos → shipping revenue is the signal that matters.
  • 2026 memory shortage path. IDC sees smartphone units down 12.9% this year.
  • Any AI capex deceleration signal. The single largest swing factor either direction.
