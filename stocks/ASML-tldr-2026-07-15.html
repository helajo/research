01

The Thesis

ASML is the sole global supplier of EUV lithography — the technology every advanced logic and memory chip must pass through — riding an AI-driven capex supercycle that just made memory customers larger than logic customers for the first time in company history.

EUV monopoly, not a market-share story

~94% overall lithography share, ~100% in EUV. 15–20 year engineering head start, exclusive Zeiss optics relationship, owned Cymer light sources. No credible competitor within a decade.

Memory now exceeds logic for the first time

Q1 2026: 51% memory vs. 49% logic system sales, as SK hynix, Samsung and Micron race to build HBM capacity for AI accelerators. Guidance raised twice in H1 2026, to €36–40B.

High-NA EUV roughly doubles ASP

$350–380M per High-NA system vs. ~$183–200M standard EUV. Modeled to grow from €1.2B (2025A) to €11.65B (2030E) — the single biggest driver of margin expansion toward management's 56–60% 2030 target.

The DCF says "expensive" — we think that's the wrong lens

A standard 5-year DCF yields just $583–790/share, far below the current price. That's a known limitation of DCF for multi-decade monopolies, not a red flag — so our target leans 75% on trading comps, only 10% on DCF.

摘要

ASML 是全球唯一的 EUV 光刻机供应商，市场份额接近 100%，短期内没有真正的竞争对手。受 AI 资本开支热潮推动，2026 年第一季度存储芯片客户的系统销售额首次超过逻辑芯片客户。High-NA EUV 新一代设备单价约为普通 EUV 的两倍，是未来毛利率提升的最大驱动力。传统五年期现金流折现模型（DCF）给出的估值远低于当前股价，但我们认为这是该模型在评估长期垄断型公司时的固有局限，而非基本面警示信号，因此估值权重更偏向可比公司交易倍数（75%）而非 DCF（10%）。

Base case: 10.6% revenue CAGR 2025A–2030E, squarely within management's own €44–60B 2030 guidance range. Bull case reaches €62B (13.5% CAGR); Bear case falls to €40B (4.1% CAGR) on an AI-capex digestion cycle plus deeper export-control curbs.

摘要

基准情形下，ASML 收入将从 2025 年的 327 亿欧元增长到 2030 年的 540 亿欧元，年复合增长率 10.6%，处于管理层官方指引区间（440–600 亿欧元）之内。毛利率预计从 52.8% 提升至 57.0%，主要受益于 EUV（尤其是 High-NA EUV）产品结构占比提升。乐观情形下 2030 年收入可达 620 亿欧元；悲观情形下若 AI 资本开支放缓叠加对华出口管制进一步收紧，收入可能降至 400 亿欧元。

Key Risks

Single-source suppliers100% of EUV optics from Zeiss; Cymer is the sole light-source maker. No viable dual-source.
China export controlsChina system-sales share fell from 36% to ~19% in one year; June 2026 US probe into a possible breach is unresolved.
High-NA execution riskUnproven at volume; a delay pushes ASP-accretive revenue and margin expansion to the right.
AI capex digestion cycleEvery prior semi capex cycle has eventually corrected; a pause would hit order intake and comps multiples together.
Customer concentrationTSMC + Samsung ≈ 38% of 2025A sales; fewer than 10 customers >80% of revenue.
Multiple compressionTrades at 46.9x EV/EBITDA — our own DCF gap means the stock is unusually sensitive to any AI-infrastructure de-rating.
摘要

主要风险包括：核心供应商高度集中（光学元件 100% 依赖蔡司 Zeiss，光源仅自有的 Cymer 一家）；对华出口管制持续收紧，中国区收入占比一年内从 36% 降至约 19%，且面临美国新一轮调查；High-NA EUV 量产爬坡存在执行风险；AI 资本开支若进入消化期将同时压制订单与估值倍数；大客户集中度高（台积电与三星合计占 2025 年销售额约 38%）；当前 46.9 倍 EV/EBITDA 的估值使股价对板块估值回调较为敏感。
