SECTION 01 · 第一节
Order Backlog & AI Supply-Demand Dynamics
定货排期视角的上涨动能：交期溢出与产能博弈
Question
From order lead times and backlog perspective — does MaxLinear have multi-year AI supply-constrained upside factors?

In the AI compute network, Broadcom and Marvell dominate PAM4 DSP. Precisely because their capacity is fully overwhelmed, lead times have stretched to 40–50+ weeks, creating a powerful second-source overflow effect for technically capable challengers like MaxLinear. Cloud customers (AWS, Meta, Microsoft) cannot tolerate a GPU cluster worth billions being stalled waiting for a $200 networking chip. This directly accelerated hyperscaler qualification of MaxLinear products and generated long-term agreements to lock in capacity.

SECTION 02 · 第二节
Why Is the Stock at $20 When 2021 Peak Was $70?
为何AI需求更强，股价却远低于2021年高点？
Question
In December 2021, demand wasn't this strong yet the stock was $70. Now demand is this strong but it's only $20. Is it undervalued?

The 2021 ~$77 peak was a bubble inflated by macro liquidity + pandemic tailwinds + supply chain panic — with no connection to AI. Broadband demand exploded from WFH; customers double-booked orders in the chip shortage panic; zero interest rates compressed discount rates to zero. None of that applies to the AI thesis.

The current $20 reflects a genuine catastrophe in the legacy business: the 2021 inventory binge led to a painful 2023–2024 destocking cycle. Telecom operators slashed capex. FY2024 total revenue fell ~48% YoY to ~$360M — broadband and connectivity revenue down 40–60%. The company swung from high profitability to a net loss exceeding $200M in 2024. The failed Silicon Motion acquisition (costly breakup fees, legal costs, management distraction) further eroded Wall Street trust.

SECTION 04 · 第四节
Hyperscaler Partnership — How Likely?
大厂宣布跟他合作的概率大吗？

A formal PR announcement from Meta, Microsoft, Google, or AWS directly naming MaxLinear is very unlikely — but this does not mean they aren't massively using the product. Cloud giants buy optical modules and server racks, not DSP chips directly. MaxLinear's direct customers are module makers (Innolight, Eoptolink, Coherent). The hyperscaler supply chain is deliberately opaque.

Instead, "partnerships" surface through: (1) Tier-1 module makers citing "MaxLinear Keystone/Rushmore DSP" in spec sheets at OFC trade shows; (2) MaxLinear appearing on a hyperscaler's Reference Design/AVL (Approved Vendor List) for new AI rack generations; (3) TechInsights teardown reports confirming MaxLinear chips inside deployed AI modules.

The 2026 guidance of 4–6M DSP shipments could not be issued without at least one North American tier-1 cloud hyperscaler (likely Meta or AWS) quietly doing high-volume primary procurement behind the scenes. Watch OFC spec sheets, supply chain analyst channel checks, and MaxLinear earnings call phrasing around "specific hyperscale customer order backlog."
中文摘要
大厂直接官宣与MaxLinear"战略合作"的概率非常低——这是由B2B半导体供应链的"潜规则"决定的。云巨头买的是"光模块"，不是直接买DSP芯片；MaxLinear的直接客户是中际旭创、新易盛等光模块厂。但MaxLinear给出的2026年指引（400-600万颗DSP出货量）如果没有至少一家北美顶级云巨头的默默采购，是绝对不敢给出的。应盯紧OFC展会上的技术规格书、供应链分析师的渠道调研，以及MaxLinear财报会上关于"特定超大规模云客户订单积压"的措辞变化。
