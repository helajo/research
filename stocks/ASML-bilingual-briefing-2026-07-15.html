The Thesis

投资论点

ASML is the sole global supplier of EUV lithography — the technology every advanced logic and memory chip must pass through — riding an AI-driven capex supercycle that just made memory customers larger than logic customers for the first time in company history.

中文

ASML 是全球唯一的 EUV 光刻机供应商——每一颗先进逻辑芯片与存储芯片都必须经过这项技术——目前正受益于 AI 驱动的资本开支超级周期，2026 年第一季度存储芯片客户系统销售额首次超过逻辑芯片客户，创公司历史先例。

EUV monopoly, not a market-share storyEUV 垄断地位，而非单纯的市场份额优势

~94% overall lithography share, ~100% in EUV. A 15–20 year engineering head start, an exclusive Zeiss optics relationship, and wholly owned Cymer light sources put ASML close to a natural monopoly — no credible competitor is within a decade of replicating it.

整体光刻设备市场份额约 94%，EUV 市场份额接近 100%。凭借 15–20 年的工程技术领先优势、与蔡司（Zeiss）的独家光学元件合作关系，以及全资拥有的 Cymer 光源业务，ASML 接近于自然垄断——十年内没有真正的竞争对手能够复制这一地位。

Memory now exceeds logic for the first time存储芯片销售额首次超过逻辑芯片

Q1 2026: 51% memory vs. 49% logic system sales, as SK hynix, Samsung and Micron race to build HBM capacity for AI accelerators. Management raised full-year 2026 guidance twice, to €36–40B.

2026 年第一季度：存储芯片系统销售占比 51%，逻辑芯片占比 49%，SK 海力士、三星与美光正加速扩建 HBM 产能以满足 AI 加速器需求。管理层已两次上调 2026 全年营收指引至 360–400 亿欧元。

High-NA EUV roughly doubles ASPHigh-NA EUV 单价约为普通 EUV 的两倍

$350–380M per High-NA system vs. ~$183–200M for standard EUV. Modeled to grow from €1.2B (2025A) to €11.65B (2030E) — the single biggest driver of margin expansion toward management's 56–60% 2030 gross-margin target.

每台 High-NA 系统售价约 3.5–3.8 亿美元，约为普通 EUV（约 1.83–2.0 亿美元）的两倍。模型预测其收入将从 2025A 的 12 亿欧元增长至 2030E 的 116.5 亿欧元，是管理层 2030 年毛利率目标（56%–60%）实现路径上最大的单一驱动因素。

The DCF says "expensive" — we think that's the wrong lensDCF 模型显示"估值偏高"——但我们认为这不是正确的评估视角

A standard 5-year DCF yields just $583–790/share, far below the current price. That's a known structural limitation of DCF for multi-decade monopolies, not a red flag — so our target leans 75% on trading comps, only 10% on DCF.

标准的五年期 DCF 模型仅给出每股 583–790 美元的估值，远低于当前股价。这是 DCF 方法评估数十年期垄断型公司时的已知结构性局限，而非基本面警示信号——因此我们的目标价权重更偏向可比公司交易倍数法（75%），DCF 权重仅为 10%。

02

Company & Technology Snapshot

公司与技术概览

ASML designs, manufactures, and services the machines that print microscopic circuit patterns onto silicon wafers. A modern EUV scanner — the size of a bus, over 180 tons, 100,000+ parts — fires a laser at molten tin droplets 50,000 times per second to generate extreme ultraviolet light, then projects a circuit pattern with nanometer precision. The portfolio spans three product lines: EUV (NXE/EXE family, €11.6B 2025A sales, +39% YoY), DUV (TWINSCAN, still the largest unit-count line at €12.0B, serving mature nodes), and Metrology & Inspection (YieldStar/HMI, €825M, +28% YoY). A growing Installed Base Management (service) business, covering ~6,700+ systems in the field, anchors margins with a "razor and blade" dynamic: sell a €183M+ EUV scanner, then service it for 20+ years.

中文

ASML 设计、制造并维护将微观电路图案印刷到硅晶圆上的设备。一台现代 EUV 扫描仪体积如公交车般大小，重逾 180 吨，零件超过 10 万个：以每秒 5 万次的频率向熔融锡滴发射激光以产生极紫外光，再以纳米级精度将电路图案投射到晶圆上。产品组合涵盖三大产品线：EUV（NXE/EXE 系列，2025A 销售额 116 亿欧元，同比增长 39%）、DUV（TWINSCAN，仍是出货量最大的产品线，销售额 120 亿欧元，服务于成熟制程）以及 Metrology & Inspection（YieldStar/HMI，销售额 8.25 亿欧元，同比增长 28%）。持续增长的 Installed Base Management（服务）业务覆盖全球约 6,700 多台在役设备，以"卖刀片+卖剃须刀"的模式支撑利润率：售出一台 1.83 亿欧元以上的 EUV 扫描仪后，可提供长达 20 余年的服务收入。

03

Industry & Competitive Landscape

行业概览与竞争格局

ASML operates within the semiconductor Wafer Fab Equipment (WFE) industry, currently sized at roughly $100–130B and projected to reach $150–280B by 2030 — implied by ASML's own €44–60B 2030 revenue ambition at a ~20–25% WFE share. Growth drivers: continued Moore's Law-adjacent scaling, the AI-driven capex supercycle, and government-subsidized capacity diversification (CHIPS Act, European Chips Act — over $300B in committed incentives through 2030). Structurally, lithography is far more concentrated than WFE broadly: ASML holds over 90% of lithography and effectively 100% of EUV. Nikon (~2.5% share) has exited the EUV race; Canon (~3.4%) competes at the low end and is betting on unproven nanoimprint lithography. Applied Materials, Lam Research, KLA, and Tokyo Electron are adjacent "coopetitors" rather than direct lithography rivals.

中文

ASML 所处的半导体 Wafer Fab Equipment（WFE，晶圆制造设备）行业目前市场规模约为 1,000–1,300 亿美元，预计到 2030 年将增长至 1,500–2,800 亿美元——这一区间与 ASML 自身提出的 2030 年营收目标（440–600 亿欧元，对应约 20%–25% 的 WFE 市场份额）相互印证。增长驱动因素包括：摩尔定律相关制程持续微缩、AI 驱动的资本开支超级周期，以及各国政府补贴推动的产能多元化布局（美国 CHIPS Act、欧洲 Chips Act——截至 2030 年累计承诺补贴超过 3,000 亿美元）。从结构上看，光刻设备领域的集中度远高于整个 WFE 行业：ASML 在光刻设备市场份额超过 90%，在 EUV 领域接近 100%。尼康（Nikon，份额约 2.5%）已基本退出 EUV 竞争；佳能（Canon，份额约 3.4%）主要在低端市场竞争，并押注尚未大规模验证的纳米压印光刻（NIL）技术。Applied Materials、Lam Research、KLA 与 Tokyo Electron 均为相邻领域的"合作竞争者"，而非直接的光刻设备对手。

DCF (10% weight): built on 2026E–2030E unlevered FCF, discounted at a 10.12% WACC and 2.5% terminal growth. Terminal value is 72.4% of enterprise value — mechanically high, but even the most bullish reasonable WACC/growth sensitivity ($852) stays far below the trading price, confirming this is a framework limitation, not a red flag. Trading Comps (75% weight): seven WFE peers (Applied Materials, KLA, Lam Research, Tokyo Electron, ASM International, Veeco, Onto Innovation). ASML trades at a premium on EV/Revenue (18.0x vs. 15.8x peer median) but roughly in line on EV/EBITDA (46.9x vs. 50.7x median), justified by superior EBITDA margin (38.4% vs. 35.2%) and backlog-driven revenue visibility. DCF（权重 10%）：基于 2026E–2030E 的 Unlevered Free Cash Flow 预测，采用 10.12% 的 WACC 与 2.5% 的永续增长率折现。Terminal Value 占 Enterprise Value 的比重高达 72.4%——数值明显偏高，但即使采用最乐观的合理 WACC/增长率敏感性组合（每股 852 美元），仍远低于当前交易价格，这说明差距源于 DCF 方法本身的框架局限，而非基本面警示信号。可比公司交易倍数法（权重 75%）：选取七家 WFE 同业公司（Applied Materials、KLA、Lam Research、Tokyo Electron、ASM International、Veeco、Onto Innovation）。ASML 的 EV/Revenue 倍数（18.0x）高于同业中位数（15.8x），但 EV/EBITDA 倍数（46.9x）大致持平于中位数（50.7x），这一溢价可由其更优的 EBITDA margin（38.4% 对比 35.2%）以及订单积压带来的更强收入可见度所解释。Precedent Transactions（权重 15%）：四笔半导体设备行业并购交易的 EV/EBITDA 中位数为 16.5x，可作为"正常周期"下的估值参考下限，但由于历史上从未出现过与 ASML 这种独家 EUV 特许经营权相类似的交易，此方法仅作辅助验证，权重较低。
