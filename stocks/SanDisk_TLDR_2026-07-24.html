Why Sell

1

A cycle-normalized DCF shows material downside — even in the Bull Case

Discounting our own modeled FY26E–FY30E unlevered FCF at a peer-informed 16.0% WACC gives a Base Case DCF of $465.65/share. Even the Bull Case — more NBM deals, HBF succeeds, the cycle stays tight — tops out at $738, still 54% below the tape.

2

The Street's own memory peers are pricing this as temporary

Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung — Sandisk's closest fundamental comps — trade at just 3.8x–6.4x forward P/E, compressed precisely because the market expects a huge near-term earnings ramp that can't repeat every year. Applying that same range to a generous blended NTM EPS of $141.79 yields $539–$907/share.

3

The $42bn NBM book is real — and already priced several times over

Five long-term supply deals, ~$42bn of remaining performance obligations, >$11bn of guarantees, covering >1/3 of FY27E bit production. Genuine structural improvement — reflected in our Base Case's 40.3% FY30E EBITDA margin — but not, in our view, worth ~3x our comps-implied fair value for that visibility.

4

History says every NAND supercycle ends the same way

Every prior cycle — most recently 2023–2024 — ended when producer capacity responses outran the demand that created the shortage. That response is already underway: Kioxia/Sandisk JV capex is up 41% YoY to $4.5bn, matched by Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron.

Reverse-DCF check

Solving for the perpetual growth rate the current price implies, at our 16.0% base-case WACC, returns roughly 13.8% — far above any sustainable long-run input to a Gordon Growth model (nominal GDP growth runs ~2.5%). Read correctly: the market is pricing in near-full delivery of the Bull Case indefinitely, not a cycle that ever normalizes.

Risk / Catalyst Map

Biggest risks to the SELL

  • More NBM deals land. Beyond the current five, each additional agreement pushes revenue visibility — and possibly the discount rate — further our way.
  • HBF wins a real design-in. A confirmed Nvidia/AMD/custom-silicon slot validates a wholly new demand category, not captured in legacy NAND TAM.
  • Pricing strength extends into FY27E. If AI capex keeps outrunning industry capacity, the historical bust gets delayed or muted.
  • BiCS10 ramps ahead of plan. Faster Kioxia JV yield ramp extends the cost-leadership window past our Base Case.
  • Street stays right. 18 of 23 analysts rate it Buy/Strong Buy, avg. target $2,197 — well above the tape, and moving up all year.

Watch next

  • Aug 6, 2026 — FQ4 2026 earnings. First full read on whether the 79–81% GM guide held.
  • Any new NBM agreements announced. Five signed so far; pace of the next ones is the single biggest swing factor.
  • HBF customer/product news. Named design wins vs. continued "pipeline" language.
  • 2026 industry capex data. Samsung / SK Hynix / Micron capacity adds — the historical precursor to every prior downcycle.
  • Spot NAND pricing trend. First sign of consumer/retail pricing cracking would front-run the Edge/Consumer normalization already in our model.
