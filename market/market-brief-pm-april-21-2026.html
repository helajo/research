Amazon Invests Additional $5 Billion in Anthropic; AI Startup Pledges $100 Billion in AWS Spending Over 10 Years
#2
Amazon announced Monday a new $5 billion investment in Anthropic at the AI startup's current $380 billion valuation, lifting Amazon's total stake to $13 billion with the potential to grow to $25 billion if Anthropic hits certain commercial milestones. In exchange, Anthropic committed to spending at least $100 billion on AWS technologies — including Trainium2 and Trainium3 custom AI chips — over the next 10 years. The deal materially strengthens AWS's competitive position against Microsoft Azure (OpenAI's home) and Google Cloud (Gemini). Anthropic already uses Project Rainier — nearly half a million Trainium2 chips — for Claude model training. The $100 billion 10-year commitment represents approximately $10 billion per year in cloud spend, providing AWS with a highly visible recurring revenue anchor in the AI infrastructure race.

Anthropic Claude Mythos Preview — First AI Model Withheld Over Safety Concerns in 7 Years — Cited by JPMorgan as Key AI Rally Catalyst
#3
Anthropic officially released Claude Mythos Preview on April 8 after the model's existence was exposed via a CMS misconfiguration on March 26. Rather than a public launch, Anthropic distributed Mythos Preview exclusively to over 50 enterprise technology organisations — including Microsoft, Nvidia, and Cisco — through Project Glasswing, a new AI-assisted cybersecurity initiative backed by $100+ million in usage credits. The model, internally codenamed "Capybara," represents a new capability tier above the Opus series: it identified thousands of zero-day vulnerabilities across every major operating system and browser, and is the first AI model to complete a 32-step corporate network attack simulation autonomously (3/10 attempts). It is the first time in approximately seven years that a leading AI lab has withheld a frontier model from public release due to safety concerns — specifically its cyber-offensive capabilities.

JPMorgan strategists Dubravko Lakos-Bujas and team cited Mythos as a key catalyst in today's S&P 500 target upgrade to 7,600 (from 7,200), upgrading 2026 EPS estimates to $330 from $315. Mythos is being actively traded on today via AI infrastructure names and drove the sector's relative outperformance versus the broader index.
