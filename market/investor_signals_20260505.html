Andrej Karpathy @karpathy
AI Lab Alumni
Independent angel · Eureka Labs founder · Former OpenAI co-founder & Tesla AI Director · Angel in Perplexity, Inception Labs
On approximately May 1, 2026, Karpathy published a full summary and transcript of his Sequoia AI Ascent 2026 fireside chat. He declared December 2025 as the software inflection point: his own code-writing ratio inverted from 80% human / 20% AI (November 2025) to 20% human / 80% agent by December — and he has written zero lines of code himself since. He introduced the term "agentic engineering": "the professional discipline of coordinating fallible agents while preserving correctness, security, taste, and maintainability." He also introduced a "verifiability" framework: LLMs peak in domains with clear correctness checks (math, code) and remain weak in unverifiable ones, creating "jagged" capability profiles.
Why it matters 为何重要 Karpathy naming December 2025 as the agentic threshold — and publicly endorsing the era shift — provides strong directional signal for AI developer tooling, testing/verification infrastructure, and agent orchestration plays; watch MSFT (GitHub Copilot), GTLB, and private names like Cursor, Cognition, and Poolside.
