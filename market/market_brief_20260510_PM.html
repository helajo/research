Market commentary 市场综述

Friday, May 8 was another day for the record books. The Nasdaq surged 1.71% to close at 26,247 — its first close above 26,000 — while the S&P 500 gained 0.84% to 7,398.93, just shy of the 7,400 milestone, with both indices posting their sixth consecutive weekly gain. The S&P 500 tech sector soared 3.27% on the day alone, with standout moves from Micron (+13.7%), Oracle (+13.6%), and SanDisk (+14.3%) alongside AMD's extended rally after its earnings blowout earlier in the week. The Dow, the odd one out, barely scraped positive at +0.02%, weighed down by Microsoft (-1.41%) and IBM (-1.06%), while the Russell 2000 small-cap index rose 0.76%, recording an intraday record. The 10-year Treasury yield dipped 3 basis points (a basis point is 1/100th of a percent) to 4.364%, a helpful tailwind for rate-sensitive growth stocks. VIX — the "fear gauge" for options markets — remained subdued at 17.19, suggesting investors are calm despite ongoing US-Iran tensions in the Strait of Hormuz.

#1
Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) reported Q1 2026 revenue of $10.3 billion (up 38% year-over-year), with its Data Center segment — the part of the business selling AI accelerator GPUs and server CPUs — generating $5.8 billion, a 57% jump from a year ago. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS, the profit per share) came in at $1.37 versus the $1.25 Wall Street expected. The company guided Q2 revenue to ~$11.2 billion, well above consensus of ~$10.5 billion. AMD's market cap — the total value the stock market assigns to the company — crossed $700 billion following the earnings release, with the stock up roughly 20% on the week and nearly 90% over the past month. The backdrop: AMD is riding the same AI infrastructure boom as Nvidia, but with some key advantages — its EPYC server CPUs dominate cloud data centres, and its Instinct AI accelerator GPUs are the only credible challenger to Nvidia's H100/H200 chips. Both OpenAI and Meta have already signed up for AMD's upcoming Helios full-rack AI system, which competes directly with Nvidia's multi-million-dollar Blackwell and Vera Rubin racks.

AAPL INTC TECH 18/20 WSJ / Management
Apple and Intel Reach Preliminary Chipmaking Deal — Intel Foundry Wins Its Most Prestigious Customer, INTC Surges 14% to a 52-Week High
苹果与英特尔达成初步芯片代工协议——英特尔代工厂赢得最重量级客户，英特尔股价单日暴涨14%创52周新高
#2
The Wall Street Journal reported Friday that Apple and Intel have reached a "preliminary chip-making agreement" that would see Intel manufacture chips for Apple devices — the first time Apple would use an American foundry (a chip fab — a factory that manufactures semiconductor chips) rather than Taiwan's TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.) for advanced chips. Intel surged 14% ($15.30) to a new 52-week high of $130.57; Apple added 2%. The deal is significant for several reasons: Apple is by far the world's most demanding chip customer (its Apple Silicon chips pushed TSMC to its limits), and winning it would be the biggest commercial validation of Intel's beleaguered Foundry division — which has burned billions of dollars trying to catch TSMC's manufacturing technology. The deal was reportedly facilitated by the Trump administration, with President Trump personally participating in talks as part of his push to reshore semiconductor manufacturing to the United States under CHIPS Act-aligned industrial policy. Microsoft, Amazon, and Tesla had previously already committed to using Intel Foundry, making Apple the fourth tech giant to back Intel's domestic manufacturing push.
Wall Street Journal / TheStreet · 8 May 2026 · Still active: both stocks remain elevated · Read more (TheStreet) · CNBC
Bull case 看涨理由
If Apple manufacturing wins real volume at Intel Foundry, it validates Intel's $30B+ process technology investment, attracts more customers, and represents a structural turning point for the company's multi-year turnaround story.
Bear case 看跌理由
The agreement is explicitly "preliminary" — Apple's chip volumes are enormous and Intel's 18A manufacturing process must perform flawlessly at scale before the deal becomes binding, which is far from guaranteed.
Short term 短期: Positive sentiment for Intel and broader US semiconductor supply chain stocks; watch for follow-on analyst upgrades.
Long term 长期: If confirmed and executed, this could add $10B+ in annual foundry revenue to Intel within 3–5 years — a transformational shift from TSMC reliance.
  • Which specific chip nodes or products is Apple planning to have Intel manufacture — A-series or M-series Apple Silicon, or lower-complexity chips like Wi-Fi and Bluetooth controllers?
  • Can Intel's 18A process node — which has had historical yield and reliability problems — meet Apple's notoriously strict quality and volume requirements for consumer devices?
  • How does this deal affect TSMC (TSM) — does Apple reducing TSMC dependence meaningfully impact TSMC's revenue outlook, given Apple represents roughly 25% of TSMC's revenue?

#3
The US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) reported Friday morning that the economy added 115,000 non-farm payroll jobs in April 2026, nearly double the Wall Street consensus forecast of 65,000. The unemployment rate held steady at 4.3%. Job gains were concentrated in health care, transportation and warehousing, and retail trade, while federal government employment declined — consistent with ongoing DOGE (Department of Government Efficiency) workforce reductions. The report directly challenged the bear narrative that the US-Iran war and oil price shock (WTI is at ~$95/barrel) were derailing the economy. As Chris Zaccarelli, CIO of Northlight Asset Management, put it: "The economy is so much better than what the doom crew has been saying. There are a lot of headwinds… and yet the labor market is adding jobs." The report is critical context for the Federal Reserve's next rate decision — a resilient labor market reduces the urgency for the Fed to cut rates (lowering borrowing costs), which means rates may stay "higher for longer," a scenario that can be a mixed signal for stocks.

美光科技周五大涨13.7%，创历史新高，本周累涨25%，受益于AI存储需求爆发和新SSD产品发布。
Oracle (ORCL) & SanDisk (SNDK)
Oracle surged 13.56% and SanDisk jumped 14.27% on Friday — CNBC's Jim Cramer had flagged these two as "the tells of this market," meaning their price action reveals the strength of the AI infrastructure trade. Both stocks are hitting 52-week highs on AI data demand.
甲骨文和闪迪周五均大涨13–14%，均创52周新高。这两只股票被市场视为AI基础设施需求的"温度计"。
Tesla (TSLA) Recall + Model Y
Tesla gained 2% to $420.17 despite recalling 218,868 US vehicles for a software glitch that can delay the rearview camera by up to 11 seconds — a fixable issue via over-the-air software update. Separately, the 2026 Tesla Model Y was the first vehicle to meet NHTSA's new advanced driver-assistance benchmark.
特斯拉股价上涨2%，尽管召回约21.9万辆汽车修复倒车摄像头软件故障（可远程更新修复）。2026款Model Y是首款达到美国NHTSA新版辅助驾驶标准的车型。
Rocket Lab (RKLB) & IREN
Rocket Lab USA soared 24.4% after beating Q1 revenue estimates; IREN Ltd. gained 8.54% after announcing a partnership with Nvidia for AI data centre operations. Both signal broadening of the AI infrastructure trade beyond traditional semiconductors into adjacent sectors.
火箭实验室（Rocket Lab）因Q1营收超预期大涨24.4%；IREN Ltd.因与英伟达合作建设AI数据中心涨8.5%。AI基础设施投资主题正从芯片向更广泛领域延伸。
