Amazon invests an additional $5 billion in Anthropic, secures 10-year $100 billion AWS commitment in return
#1
Amazon announced on Monday April 20 that it is investing $5 billion more into Anthropic — the maker of the Claude AI models — with up to a further $20 billion contingent on commercial milestones, bringing Amazon's total potential Anthropic stake to $33 billion (following an earlier $8 billion commitment). In exchange, Anthropic has pledged to spend more than $100 billion on Amazon Web Services technologies over the next ten years, including current and future generations of Amazon's Trainium custom AI chips, and has secured up to 5 gigawatts of compute capacity for training and deploying Claude models. The deal underscores the extraordinary scale of AI infrastructure spending: Anthropic's annualised revenue tripled from roughly $9 billion at end-2025 to more than $30 billion by 2026, driven by "a sharp rise" in consumer and enterprise Claude usage. Amazon made this Anthropic bet just two months after separately committing up to $50 billion to OpenAI, making AWS the de-facto backbone for both leading AI labs.

AAPL 18/20 Primary — company announcement
Tim Cook to step down as Apple CEO on September 1; hardware engineering head John Ternus named successor
#2
Apple announced on Monday April 20 that CEO Tim Cook, 65, will transition to Executive Chairman on September 1, 2026, ending a nearly 15-year tenure during which Apple's market cap grew from ~$350 billion to over $3 trillion. His successor is John Ternus, currently SVP of Hardware Engineering, a 25-year Apple veteran who oversaw the M-series silicon transition — the most consequential hardware pivot of the past decade. The board approved the succession unanimously following "a thoughtful, long-term succession planning process." The announcement follows months of pressure on Apple: CEO Cook warned in the most recent earnings call that tariffs could add $1.1 billion to costs in Q4 alone, Apple shares are down ~6% in the past month, and the company lags Mag7 peers in publicly monetised AI features. Ternus's background in silicon and hardware design signals Apple may be about to accelerate its AI chip ambitions — the rumoured "Apple Intelligence" silicon and its relationship with Anthropic (Apple is in the Claude Mythos Project Glasswing coalition) are likely to define the Ternus era.
