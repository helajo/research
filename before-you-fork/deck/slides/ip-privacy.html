Before you fork

Our boss asked about hiding your IP from strangers. Your IP address is like a return address on every letter you send: every website and API sees it, and it reveals roughly where you are and who your provider is. A VPN replaces it with the VPN’s address, but you are trusting the VPN company instead, cookies and logins still identify you, and free VPNs may make money from your data. On public Wi-Fi, most sites already use HTTPS, so the simpler habit is your phone’s hotspot for anything sensitive. The bigger leaks are usually elsewhere: a laptop server open to the network, the email address in every GitHub commit (set git to your noreply address before your first commit; past commits keep the old one), and screenshots or logs that show keys and internal details.
