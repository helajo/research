Real cases

This happens to big, trusted projects

Mar 2024 · xz-utils

A patient insider

Someone who had won the maintainers’ trust hid a backdoor in releases of a Linux compression tool that remote logins rely on. It was found by chance, when an engineer noticed logins running half a second slow.

Sep 2025 · chalk, debug

One fake support email

A maintainer of popular JavaScript packages was phished. Attackers published versions of 18 packages, with over 2 billion downloads a week, that swapped crypto wallet addresses.

Sep 2025 · Shai-Hulud

A worm that spreads

Install scripts stole developers’ tokens, then used them to publish infected copies of more packages: over 500, CISA reported. A second wave hit in Nov 2025.

No victim did anything unusual. Installing was enough.

Before you fork

Three real cases, all public. xz-utils (CVE-2024-3094, reported 29 March 2024) shows a trusted insider can be the attacker; a failed login took about 0.8 seconds instead of 0.3, which is how Andres Freund noticed. The chalk and debug compromise (8 September 2025) started with a phishing email from a look-alike domain, npmjs.help. Shai-Hulud spread by itself through install scripts; its second wave in November 2025 hit 621 packages by Check Point’s count and tried to wipe the home folder when it could not steal anything. The point: “it’s a famous project with lots of stars” is not a security check.
