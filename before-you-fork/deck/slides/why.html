Where the risk is

Reading code is safe. Running it is where the risk starts.

1

Browse

Safe

Reading the page and the code on GitHub.

2

Download

Low risk

Clone, fork or ZIP. Files are copied; nothing runs yet.

3

Install & run

High risk

Code from hundreds of strangers runs with your permissions.

4

Connect

High risk

Your API keys, files, accounts and network join in.

Do the check between step 2 and step 3: after you can read the files, before anything runs.

Before you fork

Most people think “downloading” is the dangerous moment. It isn’t: a clone just copies text files. The danger starts when you install, because installing runs code, and in a modern project that code comes from hundreds of other projects. Step 4 is new with AI tools: once you connect API keys, files and accounts, a bad project can spend your money or leak your data. So the check goes in the gap between downloading and installing.
